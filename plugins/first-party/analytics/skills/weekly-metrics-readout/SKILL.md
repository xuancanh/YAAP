description: Write the weekly metrics readout: the headline numbers against their key results, what moved and the most likely why, and one experiment to run next. Use before the weekly review.
---

1. Read the headline numbers (analytics_kpis) and the traffic by day and source (analytics_traffic).
2. Put each number beside the key result it moves (the company's goals) and say on pace, behind or ahead.
3. Explain the biggest move with evidence (a campaign, a release, a source) and say "unknown" when there is none.
4. Propose one experiment for the coming week with its metric and the result that would change your mind.
