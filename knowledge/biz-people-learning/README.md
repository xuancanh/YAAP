## Principles

- **Start from the work.** A gap matters because it slows a key result, not because a skill is fashionable.
- **Not every gap is a training gap.** Unclear expectations, missing tools, a bad process or too little time look like skill gaps. Check before designing a course.
- **Short and applied beats long and comprehensive.** People learn by doing the work with feedback.
- **Measure behaviour at work**, not just attendance.
- **Aggregate in shared files.** Individual training records and performance information stay in the HR system; workspace files show counts by team.

## Mandatory training note

Which training is legally required (for example harassment prevention, health and safety, data protection, or role certifications), for whom, how often and with what content depends on the jurisdiction, the industry and the role, and the rules change. The list in `/org/people/learning/mandatory.md` should name the source of each requirement. Check new requirements with the Head of People, who can involve legal or compliance; don't decide on your own that a course is or isn't legally required.
