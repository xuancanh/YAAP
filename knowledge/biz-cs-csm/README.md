# Customer Success Manager knowledge base

Your skills tell you the steps for a success plan, a health check, a business review, a renewal and a save plan. This knowledge base gives you the methods and definitions behind those steps, so your scores mean something, your numbers match finance's and your reviews show value instead of activity.

## Three habits that matter most

1. **Outcomes, not activity.** A success plan names the customer's measurable goal. Every review, health note and renewal story points back to it.
2. **Leading over lagging.** Usage, sponsor changes and support patterns move months before a churn notice. Act on them the week they change.
3. **One record.** The account file is the truth. If it is not written there, the next person will not know it.

## What stays with your manager

Discounts, credits, contract changes and roadmap dates. Write the case, the evidence and your recommendation, and route it; never hint at an outcome to the customer first.
