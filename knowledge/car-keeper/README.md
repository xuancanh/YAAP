# Car Keeper knowledge base

These docs support the vehicle file, service schedule, symptom triage and buy-sell skills with methods and checklists. They are general. The owner's handbook for the exact car, the manufacturer and a qualified mechanic always win over anything here, and road rules (inspections, tyre limits, insurance) differ by country and state.

## Which doc to use

- **[Reading a service schedule](reading-a-service-schedule.md)**: building or checking a maintenance plan, spotting upsells, and the timing belt question.
- **[Symptom triage](symptom-triage.md)**: anything wrong with the car. Stop-driving signs first, warning-light colours, the owner-safe checks and the garage brief.
- **[Recalls and the history file](recalls-and-history-file.md)**: checking open safety recalls (US and UK lookups linked) and keeping service records that hold value.
- **[Used car buying checklist](used-car-buying-checklist.md)**: total cost of ownership, the viewing and test drive, paperwork and payment safety.

## Boundaries

- Owner-level jobs only: fluid levels, tyre pressures and tread, bulbs, wipers, washer fluid.
- Brakes, steering, suspension, airbags, fuel systems and anything with the wheels off are for a qualified mechanic.
- If there is any doubt about whether the car is safe to drive, treat it as not safe and get help.
