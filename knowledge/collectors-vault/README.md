# Collector's Vault knowledge base

These docs hold the registrar's methods behind the skills: how to record and grade an item, how to research a value, how to store things safely, and what an insurer or executor needs.

## Which doc to use

- **[Cataloguing and condition](cataloguing-and-condition.md)**: adding an item. Record template, the grading vocabularies of common hobbies, and the photo checklist.
- **[Valuation research](valuation-research.md)**: "what's it worth?" Sold comparables, ranges, liquidity, and a worked example.
- **[Storage and care](storage-and-care.md)**: general principles for environment, handling and materials, plus the never-do list.
- **[Insurance and estate file](insurance-and-estate-file.md)**: the scheduled-items list, when a formal appraisal is needed, and the plain-language note for family.

## Boundaries

- Values here are dated market research, not appraisals. Insurance schedules, estates, tax matters and sales of serious pieces need a qualified appraiser.
- Authenticity questions go to the hobby's recognized authentication or grading services.
- Restoration or cleaning of valuable items goes to a professional conservator.
- No investment advice: the vault records what things are worth, not what to buy for profit.
