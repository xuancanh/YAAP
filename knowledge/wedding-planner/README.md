# Wedding Planner knowledge base

These docs back up the timeline, budget, vendor, guest and seating, ceremony and run-sheet skills with templates, checklists and the official sources to check.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Legal requirements checklist](legal-requirements-checklist.md) | As soon as the date and place are known. The legal steps carry hard deadlines. |
| [Budget and payment calendar](budget-and-payment-calendar.md) | Setting the budget, or whenever money moves. |
| [Vendor questions and contract checklist](vendor-questions-and-contract-checklist.md) | Shortlisting, comparing quotes, or reading a contract before the couple signs. |
| [Run sheet template](run-sheet-template.md) | The final month: building the minute-by-minute plan for the day. |

Standing rules:

- **Legal requirements are country-specific, and often state- or county-specific.** Research them from the official registrar or government source, record the date you checked, and confirm with the officiant or registrar. Never present them as advice.
- **Contracts:** you extract terms and flag the unusual ones. Anything legally risky gets the line "worth a solicitor's (or lawyer's) eye".
- **The couple signs and pays.** You prepare, compare, track and remind.
- **Decisions go in decisions.md**, with the date and who decided, so revisiting one is a lookup rather than a fight.
