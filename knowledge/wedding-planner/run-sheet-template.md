# Run sheet template

The run sheet lets the couple attend their own wedding. Every row has an owner who isn't the couple, and every risky row has a "what if". Build it backwards from the fixed points.

## Fixed points first

Before writing any rows, list the things that can't move:

- ceremony start time;
- meal service time (agreed with the caterer);
- venue access time and curfew;
- sunset time on the date, if golden-hour photos matter;
- the photographer's and band's contracted hours (and their overtime terms).

## Template (runsheet.md)

| Time | What | Where | Owner | Vendors involved | If it goes wrong |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | Hair and makeup starts (person 1 of n) | Suite | H&M lead | H&M | Order list prepared; a delay pushes the couple's own slot last |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

**Day-of coordinator:** <name, phone>. They hold the sheet, the vendor contact list and the emergency kit.

**Vendor contact list:** name, role, arrival time and mobile number, copied from vendors.md.

## Planning rules of thumb

- **Hair and makeup:** do the maths per person. People × time each, plus a buffer, worked backwards from when the couple needs to be dressed. It almost always starts earlier than people expect.
- **Photos:** agree the group-shot list with the photographer in advance, and name a person who knows the families to gather each group.
- **Travel time:** add a buffer to every transfer. Include walking from the car to the room.
- **Speeches:** decide whether they come before or during the meal, check with the caterer, and give each speaker a time limit.
- **Breathing room:** schedule 10–15 minutes after the ceremony for the couple to have a moment alone.
- **Vendor meals:** schedule them when the vendors are least needed, usually during the main course.
- **Last orders and leaving:** time these against the curfew, and include transport home for guests.

## Worked example: backwards from a 14:00 ceremony

Fixed: ceremony 14:00, meal 17:00, curfew 23:30, sunset 20:45.

| Time | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 08:00 | Hair and makeup: 4 people at 45 min each, so the couple finishes by 11:15 | H&M |
| 11:30 | Photographer arrives: getting-ready shots, details | Photographer |
| 12:30 | Get dressed | Maid of honour |
| 13:00 | Leave for the venue (20-min drive plus buffer) | Driver |
| 13:30 | Guests arrive; ushers seat them | Ushers |
| 13:50 | Registrar meets the couple privately (as the registrar requires) | Coordinator |
| 14:00 | Ceremony (30 min) | Registrar |
| 14:30 | Confetti, then 10 min alone for the couple | Coordinator |
| 14:45 | Drinks reception; group photos (list of 10 groups) | Photographer + family gatherer |
| 16:30 | Guests called to dinner | Venue manager |
| 16:50 | Couple's entrance | DJ |
| 17:00 | Meal service | Caterer |
| 18:30 | Speeches (3 × 5 min) | Best man (MC) |
| 19:15 | Room turnaround; evening guests arrive | Venue |
| 20:15 | Golden-hour portraits (20 min) | Photographer |
| 20:45 | Cake cutting, first dance | DJ |
| 23:00 | Last song | DJ |
| 23:30 | Venue curfew; taxis booked | Coordinator |

Check: the photographer's contract covers 11:30–21:00. That's 9.5 hours, so check whether that's inside their package or triggers overtime. Flag it to the couple now, not on the day.

## The couple's version

A separate sheet in large type with only their times: getting ready, leaving, ceremony, photos, entrance, speeches, first dance and leaving. No logistics. The machinery stays with the coordinator.

## Emergency kit (named bag, named keeper)

- Sewing kit, safety pins, stain remover pen, double-sided tape
- Plasters, tissues, painkillers (adults choose their own), any personal medications
- Phone chargers and a power bank
- Printed run sheet and vendor contact list
- Cash for tips, where customary
- Umbrellas, if there's any chance of rain

## After the day

- Final vendor payments and any returns of hired items
- Thank-you notes list, from the gift log
- Certified copies of the marriage certificate, and the name-change checklist if wanted

## Common mistakes

- The couple as owner of any row.
- No buffer between hair and makeup and leaving.
- Group photo lists agreed but no one assigned to gather people.
- Speeches with no time limits.
- Forgetting the vendors' contracted hours.
