# Vendor questions and contract checklist

Quotes that look alike often differ in the details: hours, staffing, overtime, and what happens if something goes wrong. Ask the same questions of every shortlisted vendor in a category, so you can compare them line by line.

## Questions for every vendor

- Are you available on our date, and do you hold dates without a deposit? For how long?
- What exactly is included: hours, staff, items, setup and pack-down?
- What costs extra: overtime, travel, meals, service charges, taxes?
- What's the payment schedule, and is the deposit refundable?
- What happens if **we** cancel or postpone? And if **you** can't make it: who replaces you, and how are they chosen?
- Do you have public liability insurance? Does the venue require proof of it?
- Can we see full examples of past work (whole galleries or full sets, not just highlights)?
- Who will actually be there on the day?

## Category-specific questions

**Venue**
- Capacity (seated, and standing), curfew, noise limits, and what happens if it rains
- Exclusive use, or other events on the same day?
- Required suppliers (an in-house caterer only?), corkage, decor rules (candles, confetti)
- Is it licensed for legal ceremonies, if the ceremony is there? (See [Legal requirements checklist](legal-requirements-checklist.md).)
- Accessibility: step-free access, accessible toilets, parking

**Caterer**
- Price per head and the minimum numbers. When is the final headcount due?
- Dietary and allergy handling. How do they label and separate food?
- Staffing ratio for the service style. Are drinks service and cake cutting included?
- Is there a tasting, and is it included?

**Photographer or videographer**
- Hours of coverage, a second shooter, and overtime rate
- Delivery time and format, the number of edited images, and the online gallery period
- Usage rights: can the couple print and share freely? Will the vendor use the images in marketing?
- Backup equipment, and how files are backed up

**Music (band or DJ)**
- Set length and breaks, plus a playlist or do-not-play list
- Power and space needs, and the venue's sound limits
- Is a microphone provided for speeches?

**Florist and decor**
- What's included in setup and collection, and when are hired items collected?
- Substitution policy if specific flowers aren't available

## Contract checklist: extract into vendors.md

For each signed or draft contract, record:

- [ ] Parties, date, venue and times
- [ ] Deliverables, specifically: hours, items, quantities, staff
- [ ] Price, what it includes, and taxes and service charges
- [ ] Payment schedule with amounts and due dates (copy into payments.md)
- [ ] Deposit: refundable or not, and under what conditions
- [ ] Cancellation and postponement terms for each side, with any sliding scale of charges
- [ ] Overtime rates and how overtime is agreed on the day
- [ ] Substitution clause: can they send someone else?
- [ ] Liability limits and insurance requirements
- [ ] Force majeure (events outside anyone's control) and what it covers
- [ ] Image or content usage rights, for photo and video
- [ ] Deadlines the couple has to meet (final numbers, timeline, song list)

## Flag for the couple when you see

- Non-refundable payments that are large compared with the total, very early in the schedule
- Vague deliverables ("approximately", "up to") on the things that matter
- No substitution plan, or the vendor can substitute without the couple's approval
- One-sided cancellation terms
- Automatic price rises or fuel surcharges without a cap
- Liability limits that cut out most remedies

For anything that looks legally risky, or if the couple is unsure what a clause means, suggest that a solicitor or lawyer reads it before signing. You extract and explain the plain meaning. You don't give legal opinions.

## T-2 weeks reconfirm message (template)

```
Hi <name> — confirming the details for <date>:
Arrival <time> at <address>, contact on the day: <name, phone>.
Service: <deliverable summary>. Balance <amount> due <date>.
Anything you need from us? Thanks!
```

## Common mistakes

- Comparing headline prices without comparing hours and inclusions.
- Not checking the venue's rules before booking other vendors.
- Leaving the cancellation clause unread until it's needed.
- Agreeing changes by phone without confirming them in writing afterwards.
