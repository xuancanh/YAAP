# Rent arrears letters

Most arrears end with the first friendly message. The letters below escalate in tone only as far as needed, and every one is written as if a judge might read it later.

Formal notices to end a tenancy have strict legal forms and timings that vary by jurisdiction. These templates are not notices. Before any formal notice, the user should check the official guidance or take legal advice.

## Day 3: friendly reminder

> Hi [name], just a quick note: I haven't seen this month's rent of [amount], due on [date], come through yet. It may just be a timing thing. Could you let me know when it's on its way? Thanks!

## Day 10: direct letter

> Dear [name],
>
> Rent of [amount] due on [date] for [address] is still outstanding. I sent a reminder on [date].
>
> If something has changed and you're finding it hard to pay, please tell me. It's much easier to sort out a plan early. Could you either pay the outstanding amount or contact me by [date] so we can talk?
>
> Kind regards,
> [landlord]

## Day 21+: formal arrears letter

> Dear [name],
>
> **Rent arrears: [address]**
>
> According to my records, the following rent is unpaid:
>
> | Due date | Amount due | Paid | Outstanding |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | [date] | [amount] | [amount] | [amount] |
> | **Total** | | | **[total]** |
>
> Your tenancy agreement (clause [x]) requires rent of [amount] on the [day] of each month. I wrote to you on [dates].
>
> Please pay the total by [date, a reasonable period], or contact me before then to agree a payment plan. If I don't hear from you, I'll need to consider the formal steps open to me under the tenancy and the law.
>
> Free, confidential debt advice is available from [local or national debt advice service].
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [landlord]

Including a pointer to free debt advice is good practice and, in some places, expected before formal action.

## Payment plan confirmation

> Dear [name],
>
> Thanks for talking today. As agreed:
> - Arrears of [total] will be paid at [amount] per [week/month], on top of the normal rent, starting [date].
> - The arrears will be cleared by [date].
> - If a payment is going to be late, you'll let me know before it's due.
>
> I'll confirm each payment as it arrives. Please reply to confirm this matches your understanding.

Make the plan affordable; a plan that fails in month two helps no one.

## Tone rules

- Facts and dates, never accusations or guesses about why.
- No threats, no informality that undercuts ("mate, pay up").
- Don't threaten actions that aren't lawful (changing locks, cutting utilities, removing belongings). They're illegal in many places.
- Don't discuss arrears with neighbours, employers or family.
- Every message goes in the communication log with the date.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting two months before the first message.
- Accepting part payments without recording them precisely.
- Mixing rent arrears with other complaints in one letter.
- Serving a formal notice from a template found online, without checking the current law.

## When to get help

- If the tenant disputes the amount, raises repairs as the reason for withholding, or mentions discrimination.
- Before any formal notice or court application: official government guidance, a landlord association's advice line, or a solicitor/attorney.
- Tax treatment of unpaid rent: the user's accountant.

## Worked example

A tenant's rent is unpaid on day 3. The friendly reminder goes out and is logged. On day 8 the tenant replies that their hours were cut. The landlord offers a call, agrees a plan that clears the arrears over four months, and sends the confirmation. Each payment is marked in the ledger. No formal letter was needed.
