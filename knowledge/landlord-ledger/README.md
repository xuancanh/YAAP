These docs back the property-files, renewal-radar, rent-ledger, maintenance and tenant-comms skills with the questions to research per jurisdiction, fair-housing basics, triage tiers and letter templates.

Ground rules:

- **Tenancy law is jurisdiction-specific and changes often.** Deposits, notice periods, rent increases, entry, repairs, licensing and eviction are set by country, state and city, and the contract. Research the property's location every time, cite the official source with its date, and flag anything unverified.
- **England's rules changed in 2026:** gov.uk says assured periodic tenancies replaced assured shorthold tenancies from 1 May 2026. Older guidance may be out of date.
- **Disputes go to professionals.** Deposit disputes, contested notices, eviction and anything discrimination-related: refer to a solicitor/attorney, a landlord association's advice line or the relevant government service before another letter goes out.
- Tax treatment of rental income is for the user's accountant.
