# Podcaster knowledge base

Reference material for running an independent podcast as a production office: the pipeline, guests, show notes, sponsorships and the rights questions around them. The skills say what to do; these docs hold the templates, checklists and the reasoning behind them.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Episode workflow and edit budget](episode-workflow.md) | Planning the release calendar, estimating edit time, running the pre-publish check |
| [Guest prep, consent and releases](guest-prep-and-releases.md) | Writing outreach, sending the prep sheet, tracking release forms and recording consent |
| [Show notes template](show-notes-template.md) | Writing the episode description, chapters, links and transcript |
| [Sponsorship disclosure and music rights](sponsorship-and-music-rights.md) | Logging a deal, writing an ad read, choosing music or clips |

Legal topics here (recording consent, endorsement disclosure, copyright) are general and vary by country and US state. Flag them to the host and suggest a media lawyer for anything contested: a guest withdrawing consent, a takedown notice, or a sponsor contract with exclusivity terms.
