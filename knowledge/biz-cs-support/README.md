# Support Specialist knowledge base

Your skills cover triage, replies, bug reports and help articles. This knowledge base gives you the priority matrix, writing patterns, templates and metric definitions behind them.

## Which doc to use

| You are about to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Set priority or decide which SLA clock applies | [Priority and SLA guide](priority-and-sla.md) |
| Write or review a reply | [Reply writing guide](reply-writing-guide.md) |

| Write or fix a help article | [Help article standards](help-article-standards.md) |
| Report response time, SLA, CSAT or reopen rate | [Support metrics](support-metrics.md) |

## Rules that never bend

- **Never promise what the company has not decided**: refunds, credits, roadmap dates, exceptions. Say who will decide and when you will update the customer.
- **Never share one customer's data with another**, including in examples, screenshots or bug reports that leave the support team.
- **Verify identity** before account changes, following the company's procedure.
- **Replies are drafts** for review unless sending has been granted.

## The quality bar for every ticket

The customer gets a correct answer the first time, in words they understand, with the next step clear, and the ticket record lets anyone else pick it up.
