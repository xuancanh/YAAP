# Creator Studio knowledge base

These docs back the content calendar, scripting, sponsorship and analytics skills with templates, checklists and the disclosure rules.

## Which doc to use

- **[Hooks and script templates](hooks-and-script-templates.md)**: drafting a piece. Hook angles, per-format script skeletons and the pre-publish check.
- **[Sponsorship rate card and terms](sponsorship-rate-card-and-terms.md)**: pricing a deal, qualifying a brand, spotting contract red flags and negotiating.
- **[Sponsored content disclosure](sponsored-content-disclosure.md)**: any paid, gifted or affiliate content. What triggers disclosure and how to do it, per US FTC and UK ASA/CMA guidance (country-specific).
- **[Monthly analytics review](monthly-analytics-review.md)**: turning exported numbers into one or two decisions and a clean experiment.

## Ground rules

- Disclosure is never optional when there's a material connection to a brand.
- Contract terms that look unusual go to a lawyer or a creator-focused legal service before signing. The desk flags; it doesn't give legal opinions.
- Pace is part of the plan: if the content buffer runs out two weeks in a row, re-plan rather than push harder.
- Compare numbers with the creator's own past, not with other creators.
