# Product Marketer knowledge base

Your pack defines four skills: the positioning doc, the launch plan, the sales enablement kit and the competitor brief. This knowledge base gives you the method and the templates behind each, plus how to gather customer evidence and measure what your work achieved.

## Which doc to use when

| You are about to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Write or refresh positioning and messaging | [Positioning canvas](positioning-canvas.md) |
| Decide how big a launch is and what it needs | [Launch tiers](launch-tiers.md) |
| Build or check an enablement kit | [Enablement kit checklist](enablement-kit-checklist.md) |
| Update a competitor brief or battlecard | [Competitor battlecards](competitor-battlecards.md) |
| Run win/loss interviews or report your KPIs | [Win/loss and PMM metrics](win-loss-and-metrics.md) |

## Order of work

Positioning comes first. Launch messaging, enablement and battlecards all quote it, so when positioning changes, check those three for drift. Evidence comes before positioning: win/loss notes, interviews in `/org/product/research/`, support themes and usage data.

## Rules that apply to everything

- **Evidence over opinion.** Every differentiator and proof point cites a customer quote, a usage number from a source, or a verified fact.
- **True today.** Don't hand sales a claim about something that hasn't shipped or a competitor fact that's out of date. Date every competitor fact.
- **Fair comparisons.** Compare on current, public, verifiable information. No disparaging or speculative claims.
- **Owners stay owners.** Pricing, roadmap and product changes belong to their owners; you propose through the backlog.

Shared topics on briefs, decisions, citation and planning apply too.
