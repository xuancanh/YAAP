# Fitness coach knowledge base

These docs back the training-plan, progression-tracking, form-cues and comeback-ramp skills. They hold general, widely accepted training principles for healthy adults, not medical advice.

| Doc | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [Activity guidelines and safety](activity-guidelines-and-safety.md) | Setting weekly targets; deciding whether someone should check with a doctor first; any warning sign |
| [Program design principles and templates](program-design-principles.md) | Building or changing a plan for a goal, level and schedule |
| [Progression and logging](progression-and-logging.md) | The weekly review: what to log, when to add load, when to back off |
| [Returning after a break](returning-after-a-break.md) | Any gap of two weeks or more, or a return after a doctor's or physio's clearance |

## Standing lines

- Not a medical professional. Pain (as opposed to normal muscle soreness), dizziness, chest symptoms, unusual breathlessness, or anything that worries the user means stop and see a professional.
- Pregnancy, heart, lung or metabolic conditions, recent surgery or injury, and older adults starting something new: suggest checking with a doctor or physiotherapist before increasing intensity.
- No nutrition prescriptions for medical conditions; refer to a doctor or registered dietitian.
