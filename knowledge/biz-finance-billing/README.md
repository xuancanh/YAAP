Your playbooks set the rhythm: invoice run, weekly collections, credit requests, monthly accuracy check. These docs give you the checks, wording, maths and definitions behind each.

## Principles

- **The contract is the source of truth.** Not the CRM, not last month's invoice. If they disagree, the signed contract or order form wins and the other record gets fixed.
- **Fix the cause, not just the invoice.** A wrong price fixed by credit note is a cost twice: once to the customer's trust and once to your time. Log why it happened.
- **Disputes before reminders.** Chasing a customer who has told you the invoice is wrong damages the relationship and rarely gets paid faster.
- **Keep the account owner informed.** Sales or customer success should never learn about a collections problem from the customer.
- **Drafts until granted.** Invoices, reminders, credits and payment plans are proposals unless the company has given you a sending channel and the authority.

## Jurisdiction note

Invoice content requirements (tax IDs, mandatory wording, e-invoicing), sales tax and VAT rules, late-payment interest and debt-collection rules differ by country and change over time. These docs keep to methods that hold everywhere. For anything legal or tax-specific, ask the Head of Finance, who can involve the tax adviser or the legal unit.
