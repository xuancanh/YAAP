# Restaurant Ops knowledge base

Methods and references for the numbers-and-people side of a small restaurant, café or food truck.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Menu engineering method](menu-engineering-method.md) | Classifying dishes by margin and popularity before a menu change |
| [Food cost math](food-cost-math.md) | Costing a plate, computing the weekly food-cost percentage |
| [Food safety and allergen records](food-safety-records.md) | Setting up temperature logs, allergen information and renewal reminders |
| [Rotas and supplier routines](rotas-and-suppliers.md) | Building the weekly rota, checking deliveries and invoices, replying to reviews |

Food-safety rules, licensing and employment law are set locally. The food-safety doc quotes the US FDA Food Code and UK law and guidance as reference points, but the local authority's requirements are what count. Send employment questions (breaks, minimum wage, contracts) to a local professional or the government's official guidance.
