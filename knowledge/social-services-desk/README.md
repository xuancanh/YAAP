# Social Services Desk knowledge base

Methods and templates for the paperwork and coordination around social work, case management and support work. The professional makes every judgment: risk, safeguarding, eligibility, case direction. This desk organises and drafts for their revision, and it is never the system of record.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Objective case-note writing](objective-case-notes.md) | Drafting a note from a contact for the professional to revise |
| [Resource directory method](resource-directory-method.md) | Adding, verifying or auditing local services |
| [Multi-agency coordination](multi-agency-coordination.md) | Appointments, professionals' meetings, follow-through and information sharing |
| [Boundaries, crisis routing and caseload view](boundaries-and-caseload.md) | Anything that looks like a judgment call, a crisis, or the caseload getting too heavy |

Professional standards, information-sharing law and safeguarding procedures differ by country, state and employer. Docs cite examples (NASW in the US; DfE guidance in England); the professional's own agency policy and regulator are binding.
