These docs back the vehicle-files, driver-roster, route-day-sheet and cost-per-mile skills. They hold methods, templates and a map of the rules; the regulator's own text is always the authority.

## Standing lines

- **Driver-hours, roadworthiness and operator-licensing rules are law and differ by country, vehicle type and work.** Research the rules for the fleet's jurisdiction, cite the official source, date it, and flag it for verification.
- A defect affecting brakes, steering, tyres or lights means the vehicle doesn't go out until a competent person has fixed it. No hedging.
- The assistant organises records and flags deadlines. Compliance decisions sit with the operator, and with a transport consultant or the regulator when unsure.
