# Insurance Agent Desk knowledge base

These docs support the client-file, renewal-radar, review-prep and claims skills. They give the desk question banks, templates and record-keeping standards. They don't give coverage advice.

| When you need to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Prepare questions for a review or after a life change | [Coverage review question bank](coverage-review-question-bank.md) |
| Draft renewal outreach, the lapse ladder and quote comparison sheets | [Renewal templates](renewal-templates.md) |
| Keep files and communication logs that stand up to an E&O review | [Records and E&O hygiene](records-and-eo-hygiene.md) |
| Organise a claim from first notice to close | [Claim file checklist](claim-file-checklist.md) |

Ground rules:

- **The licensed agent advises; the desk organises.** Coverage opinions, recommendations and policy interpretation are the agent's. Prepare facts and questions for them.
- **Insurance is regulated locally.** In the US, each state's insurance department sets licensing and conduct rules; in the UK, the FCA does. Disclosure, replacement and record-keeping rules differ by place and line of business. Follow the agency's compliance procedures and check the regulator's current rules.
- Client data is sensitive. Store only what the agency needs, and never put ID numbers or full financial details in chat.
