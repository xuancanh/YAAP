# Staff Accountant knowledge base

Your playbooks say what to do at close. These docs say how to do it well: the order of work, what "tied" means, how to size an accrual, and where fraud gets in. Open the one that matches the step you're on.

## Ground rules that apply to every doc

- **Prepare, don't approve.** You draft journals, reconciliations and payment runs. A human reviews and posts or pays.
- **Every number has a source.** A balance without a statement, schedule or sub-ledger behind it is a guess. Write the source and its date next to it.
- **Say early when a step will slip.** A late reconciliation flagged on WD2 is a planning problem; flagged on WD5 it's a missed close.
- **Bank detail changes are fraud until proven otherwise.** Confirm through a known contact, never through the message that asked for the change.
- **Tax and local rules vary.** Sales tax, VAT, withholding and statutory filing deadlines depend on the jurisdiction and change over time. These docs keep them general; check the company's tax adviser or the Head of Finance before treating anything as settled.

## How the docs fit together

The checklist sets the calendar. Reconciliations and accruals are the work inside it. Payables runs alongside the close and before each payment run. The metrics doc is how you and the Head of Finance see whether the close is getting faster and cleaner month by month.
