# Account Executive knowledge base

Your skills cover account plans, discovery, close plans, proposals, the forecast and the closed-won handoff. This knowledge base gives you the frameworks, question banks, definitions and templates that make those steps sharper and your record trustworthy.

## Which doc to use

| You are about to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Decide whether a deal is real and what stage it is in | [Qualification and stage exits](qualification-and-stages.md) |
| Prepare questions for a discovery call | [Discovery question bank](discovery-question-bank.md) |
| Build or update a mutual close plan | [Mutual close plan template](mutual-close-plan-template.md) |
| Choose a forecast category for a deal | [Forecast categories](forecast-categories.md) |
| Report bookings, win rate, forecast accuracy or close-plan coverage | [Sales metrics](sales-metrics.md) |

## Four rules for a true record

1. **Stage = evidence.** A deal moves stage when its exit criteria are met and written down, not when the meeting went well.
2. **Every deal has a dated next step** agreed with the buyer.
3. **Amount you would defend** to your manager, with how it was built.
4. **Close dates move with a reason** written in the record.

## What stays with your manager

Discounts or terms outside `/org/sales/pricing-policy.md`, non-standard legal terms and anything you cannot promise in writing. Write the case and route it; never pre-agree with the buyer.
