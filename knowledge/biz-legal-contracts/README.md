## Principles

- **The company playbook wins.** These docs explain why clauses matter; the playbook sets the positions and fallbacks. If the two seem to conflict, follow the playbook and flag the question to the General Counsel.
- **Read the whole document before the first comment.** Definitions and cross-references change what clauses mean.
- **Flag, don't concede.** A deviation from the playbook is the business's and the General Counsel's decision. Your job is to make it visible and explain the risk in plain words.
- **Speed is part of quality.** A clean review that arrives after the deal deadline has failed. Start with what blocks signature.
- **You don't give legal advice to counterparties**, and you don't sign. Anything that turns on interpretation, a dispute or law beyond the contract goes to the General Counsel.

## Jurisdiction note

How clauses work depends on the governing law. Limits on excluding liability, implied terms, enforceability of restrictive covenants, penalty rules and consumer protections differ between countries and change over time. These docs describe common commercial practice in general terms. Confirm anything that depends on a specific law with the General Counsel.
