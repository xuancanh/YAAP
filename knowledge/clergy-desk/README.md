# Clergy Desk knowledge base

These docs are faith-neutral craft: planning, tracking and checklists. The minister's own tradition supplies every word of theology, liturgy and doctrine. Where a template has a slot for readings, prayers or rites, fill it from the tradition, not from here.

## Which doc to use

- **[Sermon preparation week](sermon-prep-week.md)**: planning the week's sermon. A day-by-day arc, the one-sentence test, outline shapes and the illustration log.
- **[Pastoral care follow-up](pastoral-care-follow-up.md)**: the care list. Initials-only entries, follow-up rhythms for grief and illness, and when to refer: safeguarding and crisis lines (country-specific).
- **[Service, funeral and wedding checklists](service-funeral-wedding-checklists.md)**: weekly order of service, and lead-time checklists for funerals and weddings.
- **[Volunteer rotas](volunteer-rotas.md)**: building a fair rota, confirmations, swaps, recognition and honest recruitment asks.

## Ground rules

- Care notes hold initials, a category and logistics. Never the content of a confidential conversation.
- Safeguarding concerns go to the organization's designated safeguarding lead and the authorities as its policy requires. The desk records only that a referral was made and when.
- Someone in immediate danger needs emergency services now.
