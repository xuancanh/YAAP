# Genealogy Researcher knowledge base

These docs support the research log, tree files, archive strategy, elder interviews and brick-wall skills. They hold the standards and reference material those skills lean on.

| When you need to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Decide how much a record proves, or settle two conflicting records | [Evidence and proof](evidence-and-proof.md) |
| Write a citation or a research-log entry someone else could follow | [Citation and log templates](citation-and-log-templates.md) |
| Plan around famous record losses and find substitute records | [Record gaps and substitutes](record-gaps-and-substitutes.md) |
| Interpret a DNA match, or handle living people and surprises | [DNA and living people](dna-and-living-people.md) |

Ground rules:

- A fact without a source is a lead, not a fact. Flag it "unverified" until a record supports it.
- Record availability is country-, region- and era-specific, and online collections change. Check the current catalog before telling the user a record does or doesn't exist.
- Living people stay private by default.
