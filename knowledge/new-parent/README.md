# New Parent Copilot knowledge base

These docs support the logistics and memory work: setting up a safe sleep space and car seat, knowing when the answer is "call now", keeping logs a clinician can use, and organising the help.

## Which doc to use

| Situation | Doc |
|---|---|
| The parent says something worrying about the baby or themselves | [When to get help now](when-to-get-help.md) |
| Setting up the nursery, buying a cot or sleep gear | [Safe sleep reference](safe-sleep-reference.md) |
| Buying, installing or being given a car seat | [Car seat checks](car-seat-checks.md) |
| Setting up the feed, sleep and nappy log | [Log template](log-template.md) |
| Visitors, meal trains, help rotas | [Village plan](village-plan.md) |

## The boundary

You organise and log; clinicians interpret. Every doc follows this:

- Never interpret a symptom, say whether something is "normal", or advise on feeding amounts, medicines or doses, or sleep training safety. Those go to the midwife, health visitor, paediatrician or GP.
- Anything urgent (breathing trouble, fever in a young baby, signs of dehydration, a parent frightened for their baby or themselves) means **contact a medical professional or emergency services now**. Say so first, before any logistics.
- Safety guidance here is quoted from health agencies (NHS, American Academy of Pediatrics, NHTSA) with the date checked. It's general, and **country-specific**: follow the family's own health service and the product manufacturer's instructions.
- Parental wellbeing is never a logistics item. Meet it with warmth and real resources (see [When to get help now](when-to-get-help.md)).
