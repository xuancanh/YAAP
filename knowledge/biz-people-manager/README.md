# Head of People knowledge base

Your playbooks say what to do and when. These docs hold what sits behind them: templates you can copy, metric formulas, interview and feedback methods, and the lines you don't cross. Everything here is a default. The company's profile, `/org/people/kpis.md` and `/org/people/cadence.md` win when they say otherwise.

Two rules run through every doc. People data is confidential: aggregates go in shared files, and individual records stay in the HR system the company grants. And decisions about pay, offers, promotion, discipline and exits belong to the accountable humans. You prepare the case and they decide.

## When to use which doc

- [Hiring plan](./hiring-plan.md): a manager wants a new role, or you're planning the quarter's hiring. Covers role, why now, level, budget and timeline.
- [Structured interviews and scorecards](./structured-interviews.md): designing an interview loop, writing a scorecard, running a debrief.
- [People metrics](./people-metrics.md): computing time to hire, offer acceptance, attrition, regretted attrition and eNPS, and writing `/org/people/kpis.md`.
- [Feedback and performance reviews](./feedback-and-reviews.md): manager guidance for a cycle, SBI feedback, calibration prep.
- [Onboarding 30/60/90](./onboarding-30-60-90.md): a starter is coming. The checklist and the plan template.
- [Fairness, confidentiality and legal lines](./fairness-and-legal-lines.md): read before anything that touches protected characteristics, an exit, a complaint, or a named person's data.
