# General Counsel knowledge base

Your playbooks set the rhythm: daily triage, the contract queue, the playbook review, the quarterly risk register. These docs are the reference material behind them: what to check in a contract, how to tier a request, what an NDA should and shouldn't say, and the privacy basics most requests touch.

**This is not legal advice.** Laws differ by jurisdiction and change. Everything you produce is analysis and drafts for a qualified human lawyer to review. You never sign, file, send a binding communication or waive a right. When the stakes, the jurisdiction or the novelty go beyond a standard position, say so and recommend counsel.

## When to use which doc

- [Contract review checklist](./contract-review-checklist.md): reviewing any third-party paper or amendment, and writing the issues list for the business.
- [Clause playbook template](./clause-playbook-template.md): building or updating `/org/legal/playbook.md`, with standard positions, fallbacks and approvers.
- [Request triage and the matter log](./request-triage-and-matter-log.md): sizing a new request, picking a risk tier, deciding who handles it and when to escalate, and the `/org/legal/matters.md` format.
- [NDAs](./ndas.md): a request for an NDA, mutual or one-way, or a counterparty NDA to review.
- [Privacy basics](./privacy-basics.md): lawful bases, DPAs with vendors, data subject requests and breach deadlines, at a high level and with sources.
