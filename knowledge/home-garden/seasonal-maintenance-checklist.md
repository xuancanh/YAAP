# Seasonal maintenance checklist

A starting point for `home/maintenance.md`. Prune it to what the home actually has, and shift it to the user's climate. Seasons here are named, not dated: in the southern hemisphere they fall in opposite months, and in tropical climates "wet" and "dry" seasons may make more sense.

## Monthly

- [ ] Test smoke and CO alarms (press the test button).
- [ ] Check heating or AC filters; clean or replace per the maker's schedule.
- [ ] Run water in rarely used taps and showers (keeps traps full, flushes stagnant water).
- [ ] Clean the extractor hood filter and dishwasher filter.
- [ ] Quick look under sinks for drips.

## Spring

- [ ] Clear gutters and downpipes after blossom and seed fall.
- [ ] Walk the outside: loose roof tiles or shingles (look from the ground or with binoculars), cracked render, gaps around windows.
- [ ] Service air conditioning or heat pumps before the hot months.
- [ ] Check outside taps and hoses for frost damage.
- [ ] Check fences, gates and decking after winter.
- [ ] Garden: feed the lawn and borders, start sowing per the region's calendar.

## Summer

- [ ] Paint, stain or seal outdoor wood during dry spells.
- [ ] Clean and treat decking.
- [ ] Trim hedges (check local rules on nesting birds before cutting).
- [ ] Check window and door seals; plan any replacements before autumn.
- [ ] Garden: water deeply and less often; mulch to hold moisture.

## Autumn

- [ ] **Book the heating service before the cold rush.** This is the item whose neglect costs most; lead with it.
- [ ] Bleed radiators and check boiler pressure (if the system has them).
- [ ] Clear gutters again after leaf fall.
- [ ] Draught-proof doors and windows.
- [ ] Have chimneys swept if wood or coal fires are used.
- [ ] Drain and store hoses; insulate or isolate outside taps where frost is possible.
- [ ] Garden: plant bulbs, rake leaves off the lawn, bring tender plants in.

## Winter

- [ ] Know where the main water stop valve is, and check it turns.
- [ ] Insulate exposed pipes in lofts, garages and outside walls.
- [ ] Keep some heat on when away during freezing weather.
- [ ] Watch for condensation and mould; ventilate kitchens and bathrooms.
- [ ] Clear snow and ice from paths safely; don't climb on roofs to clear snow.
- [ ] Garden: prune dormant fruit trees and roses as the species needs; protect pots from frost.

## Every few years

- [ ] Electrical safety check by a qualified electrician (frequency depends on country and home age; landlords may have legal schedules).
- [ ] Roof inspection by a pro.
- [ ] Replace smoke alarms at 10 years from manufacture.
- [ ] Re-seal bathroom silicone and grout.
- [ ] Drain or service a water heater per the maker's instructions.

## Tuning questions (ask once)

1. Heating type: gas boiler, oil, heat pump, electric, wood?
2. House age and type: flat, terrace, detached, timber, older solid walls?
3. Climate: frost, heavy rain, heat, humidity, wildfire or hurricane season?
4. Garden: none, balcony, small, large, trees near the house?
5. What's under a service plan already?

## Common mistakes

- A generic list nobody prunes, so real items drown in irrelevant ones.
- Logging that a check was done but not what was found.
- Booking heating service in the first cold week, when trades are busiest.

## Worked example

A ground-floor flat with a combi boiler, no garden, in a frosty climate. Pruned list: monthly alarm tests; autumn boiler service and radiator bleed; winter stop-valve check. Gutters, roof and decking are the building's responsibility, so they're noted as "ask the managing agent" instead of tasks.
