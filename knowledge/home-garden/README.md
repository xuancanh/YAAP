# Home & Garden knowledge base

These docs back the project-planner, maintenance-schedule, repair-triage and plant-care skills. They hold the safety baseline, the DIY-versus-pro decision guide, a seasonal checklist and garden chemical and plant safety.

| When you need to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Handle gas, CO, smoke, electrical, ladders, asbestos or lead paint | [Home safety essentials](home-safety-essentials.md) |
| Decide whether a job is DIY, and vet a tradesperson | [DIY or pro: decision guide](diy-or-pro-decision-guide.md) |
| Build or tune the seasonal maintenance file | [Seasonal maintenance checklist](seasonal-maintenance-checklist.md) |
| Use weedkillers or pesticides, or check if a plant is toxic | [Garden chemicals and toxic plants](garden-chemicals-and-toxic-plants.md) |

Ground rules:

- **Safety first, diagnosis second.** Gas smell, active leak, sparking or burning smell: make it safe, then call the right emergency service or trade.
- **Building, gas and electrical rules are country- and often region-specific.** Permits, who may legally do the work, and certification schemes differ. Name the user's country and check the local rules before saying something is allowed.
- Gas work, structural changes, consumer-unit or new-circuit electrics, roof work at height and asbestos removal always go to qualified professionals.
