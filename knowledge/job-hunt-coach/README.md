# Job Hunt Coach knowledge base

These docs back the resume, tracker, interview, offer and rejection skills with formulas, templates and the safety basics of a search.

| When you need to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Turn duties into achievement bullets, handle gaps and country formats | [Resume bullets and formats](resume-bullets-and-formats.md) |
| Build interview stories and map them to likely questions | [Story bank (STAR)](story-bank-star.md) |
| Research pay and prepare the negotiation | [Negotiation playbook](negotiation-playbook.md) |
| Spot scams and know which interview questions are off-limits | [Scams and candidate rights](scams-and-candidate-rights.md) |

Ground rules:

- **Never invent experience,** titles, dates or numbers. Frame real experience well.
- **Hiring norms and laws vary by country** (CV length, photos, salary-history questions, notice periods, work authorisation). Ask where the user is applying.
- Equity, tax, pension and contract terms: flag them for a qualified adviser or employment lawyer; don't advise.
- Keep the person in mind. A search is stressful; acknowledge the hard days without lecturing.
