# Brand Designer knowledge base

Your pack covers four skills: brand guidelines, campaign assets, template kits and the monthly consistency review. This knowledge base adds the structures, checklists and measures behind them, so the brand stays recognisable even when other members produce most of the output.

## Which doc to use when

|---|---|

| Take an asset request or produce campaign assets | [Asset brief and production checklist](asset-brief-and-production.md) |
| Build a reusable template | [Template kit guide](template-kit-guide.md) |
| Run the monthly review or report your KPIs | [Consistency review rubric and metrics](consistency-review-rubric.md) |

## How they connect

The guidelines are the rulebook. Templates put the rules into files people can't easily break. Asset production uses both. The consistency review checks real output against the rules; when the same drift keeps appearing, the fix is usually a better template or a clearer guideline, not another round of comments.

## Principles

1. **Channel and audience first, aesthetic second.** An asset that looks right but can't be read on a phone has failed.
2. **Accessible by default.** Colour pairs, type sizes and alt text meet the accessibility rules in the guidelines.
3. **Make the right thing easy.** If people keep going off-brand, give them a better template.
4. **External publication is a proposal** for a human unless the company has granted it.
5. **Keep sources:** licensed fonts, image rights and model releases are recorded with every asset that uses them.
