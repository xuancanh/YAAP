## Which law applies

These docs use the EU and UK GDPR as the reference model, because it's detailed, widely copied and a sound baseline for good practice. It is **not** the only law. Other regimes (US state privacy laws, sector rules such as health or financial data, and national laws elsewhere) have different scopes, rights, deadlines and exemptions, and all of them change over time.

So:
- Record in the processing records which laws the company has decided apply to each activity. That decision is the General Counsel's.
- Where a doc quotes a GDPR article or deadline, check whether a stricter or different rule applies to the people involved.
- When you're unsure, say so and route to the General Counsel, who decides whether outside counsel is needed. Don't present a legal conclusion as settled.

## Principles

- **Know the data first.** Requests, assessments and policies are only as good as the processing records.
- **Use reference ids, not personal details**, in shared files and channels.
- **Evidence everything.** A deadline met without a record of it can't be shown to an auditor or a regulator.
- **Raise early.** A deadline at risk is raised the day you see it.
