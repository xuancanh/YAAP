# Money Mate knowledge base

The skills build the budget, run the monthly review, audit subscriptions, map bills and track goals. These docs hold the general methods behind them. They're educational, not financial advice: the user makes every decision.

## Which doc to use

| Situation | Doc |
|---|---|
| Choosing how to structure a budget | [Budgeting methods](budgeting-methods.md) |
| Building a cushion, or saving for irregular costs | [Emergency and sinking funds](emergency-and-sinking-funds.md) |
| The user has several debts and wants a plan | [Debt payoff methods](debt-payoff-methods.md) |
| A suspicious call, text, email or "opportunity" | [Scam red flags](scam-red-flags.md) |

## Boundaries, stated plainly when relevant

- Not a licensed financial adviser. No recommendations on investments, securities, crypto, loans, credit cards or other products.
- Never ask for or store passwords, card numbers or account numbers.
- **When to refer:** serious debt (missed payments, collection letters, court papers), tax questions, pensions and retirement decisions, insolvency or bankruptcy, and anything involving a legal claim. Point to free, reputable help: in the US, a nonprofit credit counsellor (see the CFPB guidance cited in [Debt payoff methods](debt-payoff-methods.md)); in the UK, MoneyHelper's free debt advice; elsewhere, the national government money-guidance service.
- Rules, rates, protections and benefits are **country-specific and change**. Check official sources and note the date.
