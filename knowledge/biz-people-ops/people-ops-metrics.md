# People ops metrics

These metrics show the Head of People whether the employee lifecycle runs on time and correctly. Report counts with every percentage; people teams often have small numbers, and "50%" can mean one of two.

## Onboarding completion

> **Day-one readiness** = starters with every pre-day-one step complete by their start date ÷ starters in the period

> **Onboarding completion** = starters whose onboarding checklist was fully complete by day 30 (including the check-in) ÷ starters who reached day 30 in the period

- "Complete" means every step in the template was done, not "mostly".
- Track which steps were late, by step and by owner. That list tells you which request to send earlier.

**Quality signal**: from the day-30 check-in, the share of starters who said they had what they needed on day one. Report themes from "what was missing", grouped and counted.

## Request time to answer

> **Time to answer** = time from the question being received to the first complete answer (or a confirmed handoff to the decider for routed questions)

- Report the **median** and the **share answered within the target** set by the Head of People, by category.
- A routed question counts as answered when the person has been told who will decide and when, and the decider has it. Track the decider's time separately if the Head of People wants it.
- Exclude nothing because it was hard. Complex questions may get their own category.

**Supporting measures**
- **Repeat questions**: categories with the same question three or more times in a period. Each is a candidate for a policy or FAQ fix.
- **Reopened answers**: answers the person came back on because they were unclear or wrong.

## Payroll inputs on time

> **Payroll inputs on time** = payroll runs where the complete, approved and reconciled inputs reached the submitting human by the cut-off ÷ payroll runs in the period

- "Complete" includes all changes known before the cut-off. A change that was known but left out makes the run late or wrong, even if the file was delivered on time.
- Track **payroll corrections**: changes needed in a later run because an input was wrong or missing, with cause (late notice, missing approval, entry error).

> **Payroll input accuracy** = runs with no correction caused by an input error ÷ payroll runs

## Offboarding

Not in your KPIs, but worth tracking because of the security risk:

> **Access removed on time** = leavers whose access removal was confirmed by IT & Security by the agreed time ÷ leavers in the period

Add one change per miss: a lead time to lengthen, a template step to add, a reminder to move earlier.

## Privacy in reporting

Metrics use counts, categories and reference ids. Don't name individuals in the metrics report, and be careful with very small groups: if a category has one person, a "theme" can identify them. Merge small categories or report them only to the Head of People.
