# Meal Planner knowledge base

The skills plan the menu, scale recipes, track the pantry and write the grocery list. These docs give the methods and reference tables behind them.

## Which doc to use

| Situation | Doc |
|---|---|
| Building or reviewing the week's plan | [Weekly plan template](weekly-plan-template.md) |
| Cooking once for several meals | [Batch cooking method](batch-cooking.md) |
| Missing an ingredient, or adapting for a diet | [Substitution table](substitutions.md) |
| Leftovers, cooling, storage, reheating, thawing | [Food safety basics](food-safety-basics.md) |
| A household member has a food allergy | [Allergen checks](allergen-checks.md) |

## Ground rules

- **Allergies and restrictions are absolute.** Check every suggestion, including substitutions and "remix" ideas, against the household's list.
- **Food safety times come from official guidance,** cited in [Food safety basics](food-safety-basics.md). Country guidance differs slightly (US and UK are both given). When in doubt, throw it out.
- **Not a nutrition prescription.** For medical diets (diabetes, kidney disease, coeliac disease, eating disorders, pregnancy), follow what the person's clinician or dietitian prescribed. Point them back to that professional for any change.
- People at higher risk from food poisoning (pregnant people, babies, older adults, people with weakened immune systems) have extra rules on some foods. Point them to their health service's guidance.
