# Substitution table

A substitution has to work in the recipe and stay inside the household's constraints. Before using any swap, check it against the allergy list; some common swaps introduce an allergen (see [Allergen checks](allergen-checks.md)).

Baking is less forgiving than cooking. In a stew, most swaps are fine. In a cake, change one thing at a time.

## Dairy

| Missing | Swap | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buttermilk | Milk with a spoon of lemon juice or vinegar, rested a few minutes | Works in pancakes, scones, cakes |
| Butter (baking) | Block plant-based spread or neutral oil (use a bit less oil) | Oil changes texture; cookies spread more |
| Milk | Oat, soy or other plant milk | Nut milks: tree-nut allergen. Soy: soy allergen |
| Cream (in sauces) | Evaporated milk, or milk thickened with a little flour | Less rich; don't boil hard |
| Sour cream | Plain yoghurt | Can split if boiled; stir in at the end |

## Eggs (binding or leavening)

| Swap per egg | Best in |
|---|---|
| 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water, rested | Muffins, pancakes, dense bakes |
| Mashed banana or apple sauce (about a quarter cup) | Sweet bakes; adds flavour and moisture |
| Aquafaba (chickpea water), about 3 tbsp | Meringues, mousses, lighter bakes |
| Commercial egg replacer | Follow the packet |

Egg swaps work best in recipes with one or two eggs. Recipes built on eggs (custard, soufflé) don't swap well.

## Flour and thickeners

| Missing | Swap | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Self-raising flour | Plain flour plus baking powder (follow the pack ratio) | |
| Wheat flour (gluten-free need) | A gluten-free flour blend | Needs a binder for bread; check "may contain" for coeliac needs |
| Cornflour for thickening | Plain flour (use more) or arrowroot | Flour needs cooking out to lose the raw taste |
| Breadcrumbs | Oats, crushed crackers, gluten-free crumbs | |

## Flavour and pantry

| Missing | Swap |
|---|---|
| Fresh herbs | Dried, about a third of the amount; add earlier in cooking |
| Lemon juice | Lime juice or a little vinegar |
| Wine in a sauce | Stock plus a splash of vinegar |
| Soy sauce | Tamari (often gluten-free; check label), or coconut aminos for soy-free |
| Fish sauce | Soy sauce with a pinch of salt (not for soy allergy) |
| Shallots | Onion, a little less |
| Fresh garlic | Garlic powder, a small pinch per clove |
| Brown sugar | White sugar with a little treacle or molasses |
| Stock | Water plus a stock cube or bouillon; check cubes for gluten, celery and milk |

## Protein swaps for vegetarian meals

| Instead of | Try | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minced meat | Lentils, chopped mushrooms, soy mince | Lentils hold well in bolognese and chilli |
| Chicken pieces | Chickpeas, tofu, paneer | Tofu and soy mince: soy. Paneer: milk |
| Fish | Tofu, aubergine (for texture), beans | |
| Meat stock | Vegetable stock, mushroom stock | |

## Worked example

Recipe: banana bread, needs 2 eggs and butter. Household: egg allergy.

Swap the eggs for 2 flax "eggs" (2 tbsp ground flax + 6 tbsp water), keep the butter. Check the flaxseed packet for "may contain" warnings. Expect a slightly denser loaf. Note the result in the recipe file for next time.

## Common mistakes

- Swapping several things at once in a bake.
- A dairy-free swap that's nut-based for a nut-allergic guest.
- Stock cubes and spice mixes not checked for hidden allergens.
