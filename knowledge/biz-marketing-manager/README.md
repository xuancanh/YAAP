# Head of Marketing knowledge base

Your playbooks say what to do and when. These docs hold the definitions, frameworks and templates behind them, so the numbers you report mean the same thing every week and every brief your team gets has the same shape.

Two rules run through all of it. Never invent a number: every metric comes from `org_metric_read`, a named file or a named person, or it's labelled an estimate. And anything outward (publishing, sending, spending) is a draft for a human to approve. Every threshold and cadence here is a default the company can change in `/org/marketing/cadence.md` or `kpis.md`.

## When to use which doc

- [Marketing metrics](./marketing-metrics.md): writing or auditing `/org/marketing/kpis.md`, computing CAC, conversion and pipeline in the channel review, or when a number looks too good.
- [Positioning](./positioning.md): before a launch, when conversion or win rates stall, or when copy across channels says different things.
- [Campaign brief](./campaign-brief.md): every campaign on the monthly calendar, and every campaign task you brief to a specialist.
- [Attribution and channel experiments](./attribution-and-experiments.md): the monthly channel review, any budget shift, and any claim that a channel "works".
- [Claims and approvals](./claims-and-approvals.md): before anything goes public: claims, proof, disclosure and who signs off.
- [Weekly marketing report](./weekly-report.md): the Friday report, and month or quarter end.
