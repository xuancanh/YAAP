# Agency Recruiter knowledge base

These docs give the desk its working tools: question banks, search syntax, note templates and message drafts. The skills say what to do; these say how, with examples.

## Which doc to use

- **[Intake question bank](intake-question-bank.md)**: before and during a client intake call. Questions that separate must-haves from wish-list, the sell, and the process, plus a brief template.
- **[Boolean search reference](boolean-search-reference.md)**: building and logging search strings. Operators, patterns, a title-synonym map and common mistakes.
- **[Structured screen notes](structured-screen-notes.md)**: running a screen and writing it up. The evidence-only template, what never goes in a note, and the legal background on protected characteristics (US and UK, flagged as country-specific).
- **[Client and candidate messages](client-and-candidate-messages.md)**: the weekly client update, submission package, feedback chase, candidate status notes and rejections.

## Ground rules

- Never submit a candidate without their explicit consent for that client.
- Current-employer discretion is absolute: no approach that could expose a candidate at work.
- Notes record evidence against agreed criteria only.
- Discriminatory client requests get a clear, professional refusal. Employment law differs by country and state; when a situation is unclear, the agency's compliance lead or an employment lawyer decides.
