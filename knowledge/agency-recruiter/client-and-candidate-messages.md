# Client and candidate messages

Short, specific and on time beats polished and late. Adapt the tone to the client and the person; keep the structure.

## Weekly client update (per role)

```
Subject: [Role] — week of [date]

This week: sourced [n] · screened [n] · submitted [n] (total submitted: [n])
In process: [Name A] — 2nd interview Thu · [Name B] — awaiting your feedback since Tue
Market note: [one honest signal, e.g. "comp came up as the blocker in 4 of 6 conversations"]
Next week: [the plan, e.g. "second pass on fintech platform teams; two referrals pending"]
Need from you: [the one ask, with a date]
```

Send it before the client asks. If nothing moved, say so and say what you're changing.

## Submission package

```
[Candidate name] for [Role]  — consent to submit confirmed [date]

Why them, against the brief:
- Must-have 1: [evidence in one line]
- Must-have 2: [evidence]
- Strong preference: [evidence or gap, stated honestly]
Logistics: notice [ ] · location/pattern [ ] · comp expectation [ ]
Watch-outs: [any gap or open question]
Suggested next step: [interview format, availability]
```

Never submit without the candidate's consent for that specific client.

## Feedback chase

Day the SLA lapses:

> "Hi [name], quick nudge on [A] and [B] from Tuesday. Both are active elsewhere, so a yes/no by Thursday helps keep them. Even a one-liner on what's missing helps me calibrate."

When slowness keeps costing you:

> "We've lost two finalists to faster processes this month. Could we hold interview slots on Tuesdays and Thursdays and commit to feedback within two working days?"

## Candidate status notes

Everyone active hears from you at least weekly while something is pending, even with no news.

No news:

> "Hi [name], no decision yet on [client]. They've said end of next week. I'll update you Friday either way."

Moving forward:

> "Good news: [client] would like a second interview. Here's who you'll meet, what they'll focus on, and two things worth preparing."

## Rejection (after interview)

> "Hi [name], I'm sorry to say [client] has decided not to move forward. Their feedback: [specific, kind, job-related]. You came across strongly on [strength]. I'd like to keep you in mind for roles like [type]; is that okay?"

Record their consent to stay in touch before you add them to the silver-medalist file.

## Offer stage

Before the offer, confirm with the candidate: base, bonus, start date, and anything that would make them say no. Tell the client the candidate's real numbers so the offer lands inside them.

Counter-offer prep for the candidate:

> "Your current employer may counter. It's your decision. Worth asking yourself: would the reasons you started looking still be there with more money?"

## Placement aftercare

- First week: "How's the first week? Anything you weren't expecting?"
- Day 30 and day 90 calls, calendared. Note both dates relative to the guarantee or rebate period in the client's terms.
- An early wobble goes to both sides quickly and kindly.

## Common mistakes

- Updates full of activity and no signal.
- Ghosting candidates after a "no".
- Rejection feedback that leaks protected or personal remarks from the client. Pass on only job-related feedback.
- Forgetting to invoice on start date per terms.
