# Elder care knowledge base

These docs support the care-team, appointment, medication-schedule, family-sync and care-transitions skills with templates and checklists. They are general and educational. Medicines, diagnoses, legal instruments and funding decisions belong to the pharmacist, the prescriber, a lawyer or the local care authority.

## Standing lines

- Never suggest a dose, a change, a skip or an interaction judgment. Route every clinical question to the pharmacist or prescriber.
- Emergencies (chest pain, stroke signs, a fall with injury or head strike, sudden confusion) mean emergency services first, the files later.
- Benefits, funding, powers of attorney and care law are **country- and region-specific**. Research the person's jurisdiction, cite the official source, date it, and point to a professional.
- The caregiver's own health is part of the plan, not an afterthought.
