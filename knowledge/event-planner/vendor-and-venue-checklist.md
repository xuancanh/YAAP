# Vendor and venue checklist

Compare like with like, get everything in writing, and read the cancellation terms before paying anything.

## Venue visit questions

**Fit**
- [ ] Capacity for the format (seated vs. standing) and the realistic guest count.
- [ ] Accessibility: step-free access, accessible toilets, parking, distance from transport.
- [ ] Indoor fallback if any part is outdoors.
- [ ] Noise limits and curfew; end time including pack-down.

**What's included**
- [ ] Tables, chairs, linens, AV, staff, cleaning.
- [ ] Exclusive use or shared with other events?
- [ ] Can we bring our own caterer, cake, alcohol? Any fees for that?
- [ ] Access times for setup and vendor deliveries.

**Rules**
- [ ] Decor restrictions (candles, confetti, fixing things to walls).
- [ ] Alcohol service rules and who holds the licence. Licensing is local law; the venue should know it.
- [ ] Insurance requirements for outside vendors.
- [ ] Safety: fire exits, maximum occupancy, first-aid arrangements.

## Comparing vendor quotes

Line up quotes on the same basis:

| Vendor | What's included | Hours | Price | Service/tax | Deposit | Balance due | Cancellation | Reviews checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Ask each vendor the same questions:

- Are you available on the date, and will you hold it? Until when?
- Who exactly will turn up (the owner or a team member)?
- What happens if you are ill or your equipment fails?
- Setup and departure times; space and power needs.
- References from a similar event.

## Contract check before the deposit

Read for these, and ask about anything missing:

- [ ] Date, times, location and the exact services, quantities and names.
- [ ] Total price with every fee, tax and service charge.
- [ ] Payment schedule: amounts and due dates.
- [ ] **Cancellation and postponement terms** on both sides, including what happens to the deposit.
- [ ] Final-numbers deadline and whether numbers can go down after.
- [ ] Liability, damage and insurance terms.
- [ ] What happens if they cannot deliver (substitute, refund).
- [ ] Weather or force-majeure clause.

For large sums or unusual terms, suggest the host gets advice from a consumer-advice service or a lawyer. The assistant flags terms; it doesn't interpret them as legal advice.

## Food safety with caterers

Ask caterers whether they are registered or licensed as required where they operate, and how food is kept hot or cold during service. For home-cooked food at larger gatherings, follow the local food-safety agency's guidance on keeping food out of the temperature danger zone.

## Red flags

- No written contract, or pressure to pay in full immediately.
- Cash only, with no receipt.
- Reviews that are all recent and vague.
- Vague answers to "what if you can't make it?"

## After booking

File the contract in the event folder, add every due date to the calendar, and note the vendor's day-of contact number in the run sheet.
