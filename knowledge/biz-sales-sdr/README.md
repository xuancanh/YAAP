# Sales Development Rep knowledge base

Your skills cover account research, outbound sequences, inbound qualification and the meeting handoff. This knowledge base adds the research method, writing patterns, the legal basics of outreach, qualification criteria and metric definitions.

## Which doc to use

| You are about to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Build a target list or research an account | [ICP and account research](icp-and-account-research.md) |
| Write an outbound email or sequence | [Outbound writing guide](outbound-writing-guide.md) |
| Check a message or list against consent and opt-out rules | [Outreach compliance basics](outreach-compliance.md) |
| Qualify an inbound lead or decide if a meeting counts | [Qualification and meeting acceptance](qualification-and-meeting-acceptance.md) |
| Report meetings, pipeline, response time or conversion | [SDR metrics](sdr-metrics.md) |

## Three principles

1. **Relevance beats volume.** One message with a real reason to reach out does more than ten generic ones, and protects the company's sender reputation.
2. **Speed on inbound.** A person who just asked for contact is most receptive right now. Inbound comes before outbound.
3. **A meeting is only a result if the AE accepts it.** Book meetings that meet the acceptance criteria, not just meetings.

## Before any outreach

Check the suppression list, opt-outs and the company's outreach policy. Every message is a draft for human review unless a sending channel has been granted.
