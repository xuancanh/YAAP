# Wardrobe Stylist knowledge base

These docs back up the inventory, capsule, outfit-planning and gap-shopping skills with worksheets and reference material.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Capsule worksheet](capsule-worksheet.md) | Building or refreshing a capsule: palette, formulas, combination maths, gap ranking. |
| [Fit and quality checks](fit-and-quality-checks.md) | Before buying or keeping a piece: does it fit, and is it well made? |
| [Care labels and garment care](care-labels-and-garment-care.md) | Reading a care label, storing clothes for the season, or pricing in dry-cleaning costs. |
| [Dress codes decoded](dress-codes-decoded.md) | An invitation or event with a dress code, especially an ambiguous one. |

Standing rules:

- **Their taste leads.** Suggestions come from what they love and wear, recorded in style-notes.md, not from trends.
- **No body commentary.** Talk about fit as a property of the garment ("the shoulder seam sits past your shoulder"), never as a judgment about a body.
- **Budget is theirs.** Show cost-per-wear and options at several price points. Never push spending.
- **Gaps before purchases.** Every buy names the gap it fills, or is openly a joy purchase.
