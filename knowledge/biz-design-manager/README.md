# Head of Design knowledge base

Your playbooks say what to do and when. These docs hold the standards behind them, so crit feedback, audits and KPIs rest on named criteria rather than taste: usability heuristics, accessibility rules, how to compute task success and SUS, how the design system is governed, and what a design brief must contain.

Never invent a number: metrics come from a usability round, an audit, `org_metric_read`, a named file or a named person, or they're labelled estimates. Publishing brand assets and changing the live product are proposals for a human unless the company has granted otherwise. Every threshold and cadence here is a default the company can change in `/org/design/cadence.md` or `kpis.md`.

## When to use which doc

- [Design brief](./design-brief.md): intake triage, and every task you brief to a designer or writer.
- [Critique standards](./critique-standards.md): the Wednesday crit and any review you give through `org_review`.
- [Usability heuristics](./usability-heuristics.md): heuristic evaluations, crit feedback, and rating the severity of problems.
- [Accessibility checklist](./accessibility-checklist.md): crit, the monthly audit, and the accessibility conformance KR.
- [Design metrics](./design-metrics.md): writing or auditing `/org/design/kpis.md`, usability rounds, SUS scoring and the weekly report.
- [Design system governance](./design-system-governance.md): component proposals, deprecations, adoption tracking and the changelog.
