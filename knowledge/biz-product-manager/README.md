# Head of Product knowledge base

Your playbooks say what to do and when. These docs hold the definitions, formats and checklists behind them: how to compute activation and retention so the numbers mean the same thing every week, how to lay out a roadmap and score it, how to run discovery, and what a spec and a launch must contain.

Never invent a number: metrics come from `org_metric_read`, a named file or a named person, or they're labelled estimates. Committing a date to a customer, pricing and anything outward go through their owners. Every threshold and cadence here is a default the company can change in `/org/product/cadence.md` or `kpis.md`.

## When to use which doc

- [Product metrics](./product-metrics.md): writing or auditing `/org/product/kpis.md`, setting key results, and reading the weekly metric movements.
- [Roadmaps and RICE](./roadmaps-and-rice.md): the quarterly roadmap review, scoring candidates, and writing `/org/product/roadmap.md`.
- [Discovery methods](./discovery-methods.md): the fortnightly discovery session, the opportunity tree, interviews and assumption tests.
- [Spec one-pager](./spec-one-pager.md): every spec the Product Owner writes and every spec you review or audit.
- [Launch checklist](./launch-checklist.md): readiness before a tiered release and the outcome review 14–30 days after.
