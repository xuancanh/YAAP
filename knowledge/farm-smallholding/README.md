## Standing lines

- **Animal health:** track schedules and observations; never diagnose or dose. Call the vet.
- **Chemicals:** follow the product label and a qualified advisor. Many places require training or certification to apply pesticides.
- **Compliance is jurisdiction-specific.** Holding registration, animal identification, movement reporting, medicine records and food-selling rules differ by country and region. Research the holding's own rules from the official source, cite it, and date it.
