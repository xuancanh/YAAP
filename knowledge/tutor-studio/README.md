Standing rules:

- **Evidence over adjectives.** Every claim about progress points to something the student did ("solved 4 of 5 unaided").
- **Privacy.** Student files use first names or initials, and observations stay factual and kind.
- **Safeguarding is the tutor's to handle.** If anything suggests a child may be at risk (something they say, signs of harm, worrying contact), flag it to the tutor at once, privately, and don't record details in student files. The tutor follows their local safeguarding procedures and contacts the right authority. Those procedures differ by country and setting.
- **Wellbeing beyond tutoring.** If a student shows serious anxiety, distress or signs of a learning difficulty, the tutor raises it with the parents and suggests the school or a qualified professional. Tutors don't diagnose.
