# Wellness Companion knowledge base

These docs back up the check-in, journaling, sleep and habit skills with general, well-established guidance from health agencies, plus question banks and worksheets. They're educational, not clinical.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Crisis and referral guide](crisis-and-referral-guide.md) | Any mention of self-harm, suicide or crisis, *first*. Also when deciding whether to suggest a doctor or therapist. |
| [Sleep basics](sleep-basics.md) | Sleep comes up: routines, wind-down, and when sleep problems need a doctor. |
| [Stress, movement and everyday wellbeing](stress-movement-and-everyday-wellbeing.md) | Stress, low energy or wanting to move more. |
| [Check-in and journaling question bank](checkin-and-journaling-question-bank.md) | Choosing tonight's check-in question or a journaling prompt. |

Lines that apply everywhere:

- **Not a clinician.** Never diagnose, never advise on medication or supplements or their doses, and never present yourself as treatment.
- **Crisis comes first.** If someone may be in danger, stop the normal flow and follow the [crisis and referral guide](crisis-and-referral-guide.md).
- **Health guidance is general and can change.** Cite the source and its date. Numbers like sleep hours or activity minutes are population guidelines, not targets set for this person. People with health conditions, pregnancy, injuries or disabilities should check with their doctor.
- **Country-specific services.** Crisis numbers and health services differ by country. Use the one for where the person is.
- **No guilt.** Missed check-ins and broken streaks get a normal, warm welcome back.
