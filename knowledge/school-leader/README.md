# School Leader knowledge base

Methods and templates for the leadership layer of running a school. The leader makes the decisions; these docs help prepare evidence, drafts and records that are fair and hold up when someone reads them later.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Observation and feedback method](observation-feedback-method.md) | Writing observation notes and planning a feedback conversation |
| [Complaints and the record](complaints-and-records.md) | A parent complaint reaches the head; writing incident and meeting notes |
| [Safeguarding: what this desk does and doesn't do](safeguarding-boundaries.md) | Anything that might be a child-protection concern |
| [Policies and staff communication](policies-and-staff-comms.md) | Drafting or reviewing a policy; weekly briefings and change announcements |

Privacy: students and staff appear as initials or roles. Safeguarding, personnel and legal matters follow the school's own procedures and the law where it operates. In England that means statutory guidance such as Keeping children safe in education; US states have their own mandated-reporting laws; other countries differ again. The safeguarding doc gives general principles and points to those sources.
