# Turnover checklist

A turnover has two jobs: clean, then check. Keep them as separate passes, ideally with a fresh pair of eyes for the check.

## Runbook template (adapt per property)

**Arrival**
- [ ] Note the time; photograph anything damaged or missing before touching it.
- [ ] Open windows; start the first laundry load.

**Bedrooms**
- [ ] Strip beds; check mattress protectors and replace if stained.
- [ ] Make beds to the standard photo.
- [ ] Check under beds, drawers and wardrobes for left items.
- [ ] Dust surfaces, lamp shades; vacuum or mop.
- [ ] Hangers (set number), spare blanket, chargers if provided.

**Bathroom**
- [ ] Clean toilet, sink, shower/bath, mirrors; descale as needed.
- [ ] Check drains run freely; clear hair.
- [ ] Fresh towels to the standard (set number per guest).
- [ ] Restock toilet roll, soap, shampoo to par.
- [ ] Bin emptied and relined.

**Kitchen**
- [ ] Dishes clean and put away; check the dishwasher is empty.
- [ ] Fridge cleared of guest food and wiped.
- [ ] Hob, oven (spot check), microwave, kettle, coffee maker clean.
- [ ] Restock coffee, tea, salt, oil, dish soap, sponge to par.
- [ ] Bins emptied, recycling sorted per local rules.

**Living areas**
- [ ] Cushions and throws reset; remotes in place and working.
- [ ] Floors vacuumed and mopped.
- [ ] Welcome touches set out.

## Inspection pass (after the clean)

Stand in each doorway and look as a guest would, then:

- [ ] All lights work.
- [ ] Wifi connects; the password card matches.
- [ ] Hot water runs; heating or AC responds.
- [ ] Smoke and CO alarms pass a button test.
- [ ] Locks, key box and smart lock work; codes reset for the next guest.
- [ ] No hair, crumbs or smells; bins fresh.
- [ ] Anything found goes in the damage-and-wear log with a photo and the booking reference.

## Par list (per stay)

The par is the amount you reset to every time. Example for a two-bedroom flat; adjust to the property:

| Item | Par | Restock at |
|---|---|---|
| Toilet rolls | 2 per bathroom + 4 spare | below 4 spare |
| Coffee pods or bags | enough for 2 per guest per night | below par |
| Dish tabs | 4 | below 4 |
| Bin bags | 6 | below 6 |
| Hand soap | 1 per sink, full | under half |

Keep the reserve stock in a locked cupboard and count it at each turnover.

## Linen sets

You need enough sets that one is always on the beds, one is in the wash, and one is clean in the cupboard. For back-to-back bookings, that's at least **three sets per bed**, plus spare pillowcases and towels for stains. If laundry is sent out and takes days to return, add a fourth.

Worked example: two beds, same-day turnovers most weekends, in-house washing. Three sets per bed is six sets; add two spare towel sets per bathroom. Without the third set, a late checkout plus a wet day means making beds with damp sheets.

## Tight turnovers

- Confirm the cleaner the day before for any same-day changeover.
- Set a realistic checkout and check-in gap for the property's size.
- Keep a "rescue kit" on site: a spare made-up set, emergency toiletries and a list of who to call.

## Common mistakes

- Combining clean and inspection, so the cleaner's blind spots never get caught.
- No photo standard, so every cleaner makes the bed differently.
- Running out of a consumable mid-stay because nobody counts the reserve.
