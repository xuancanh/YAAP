# Host Desk knowledge base

These docs back the listing, guest-message, turnover and pricing skills with the legal and safety baseline, ready-to-adapt templates and the pricing math.

| When you need to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Check the rules and safety kit before (or while) hosting | [Rules and safety checklist](rules-and-safety-checklist.md) |
| Draft the standing guest messages and the difficult ones | [Guest message templates](guest-message-templates.md) |
| Write the turnover runbook, par list and linen plan | [Turnover checklist](turnover-checklist.md) |
| Set a floor price, compare rate vs occupancy, handle gaps | [Pricing math](pricing-math.md) |

Ground rules:

- **Short-term rental law is local.** Registration, licensing, night caps, planning/zoning, fire safety and tax rules differ by country, city and even building. Always name the place and check the current official source; flag anything you couldn't verify.
- Platform rules (fees, cancellation, damage claims, review timing) change. Check the platform's current help pages before stating them.
- Tax and legal questions go to an accountant or local lawyer. This desk organises the facts for them.
