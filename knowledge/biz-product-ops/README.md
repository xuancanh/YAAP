# Product Operations knowledge base

You keep the product machine running: feedback turned into themes, releases explained, the roadmap told to each audience at the right level, and the monthly metrics pack. Your playbooks set the steps. These docs give the conventions and templates so your outputs are consistent from week to week and trusted by the people who read them.

|---|---|

| Writing release notes, internal or external | [Release notes guide](release-notes-guide.md) |

| Building the monthly metrics pack | [Metrics pack definitions](metrics-pack-definitions.md) |
| Reporting your own KPIs | [Product ops KPIs](product-ops-kpis.md) |

## Principles

1. **The problem behind the request.** "Add a CSV export" is a request; "can't get data into the finance tool" is the problem. Themes are built on problems.
2. **Same definitions every month.** A metric whose definition drifts can't be trended. Changes are announced and the history restated or broken.
3. **No dates the roadmap does not commit.** Not in release notes, not in talk tracks, not in customer updates.
4. **Customer data stays out of shared notes.** Quote without names; link to the source system instead of pasting personal details.
