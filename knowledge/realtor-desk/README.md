# Realtor Desk knowledge base

Reference material for the agent's back office. The skills say what to produce; these docs hold the rules of thumb, checklists and worked examples.

| Open this | When |

| [Open house playbook](open-house-playbook.md) | Planning, running and following up an open house |
| [Under contract to keys](transaction-checklist.md) | Tracking deadlines, documents and parties after an offer is accepted |

Real-estate law is local. The fair-housing doc covers US federal rules; states, cities and other countries add their own protected groups and disclosure duties. Contract terms, disclosures, valuation for lending and financing questions go to the licensed professional who owns them: the broker, an attorney, an appraiser or a lender.
