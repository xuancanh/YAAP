# Professor Desk knowledge base

Methods, templates and checklists for the teaching-and-service side of academic work. The professor decides every grade and signs every letter; these docs help prepare drafts that are consistent and quick to review.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Backward design and the syllabus](backward-design-and-syllabus.md) | Designing a course or revising a syllabus before term |
| [Rubric design and consistent grading](rubric-design.md) | Writing a rubric, calibrating, drafting feedback |
| [Recommendation letter kit](recommendation-letter-kit.md) | A student asks for a letter; drafting in the professor's voice |
| [Student email and office hours](student-email-and-office-hours.md) | Triage categories, reply templates, running office hours |

Student privacy: use initials or roles in files. Education-record privacy laws (for example FERPA in the US, GDPR in the UK and EU) and the institution's own policies apply; the registrar or data-protection office answers specific questions. Accommodations are handled through the disability services office's process, never negotiated in email drafts.
