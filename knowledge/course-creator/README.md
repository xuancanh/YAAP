# Course Creator knowledge base

These docs give the working tools for both halves of the job: teaching that changes what learners can do, and selling that stays honest.

## Which doc to use

- **[Backward design worksheet](backward-design-worksheet.md)**: designing or revising a course. Outcomes, module ladder, lesson template and a padding check.
- **[Cohort playbook](cohort-playbook.md)**: running a live cohort. Week-by-week rhythm, message templates, the re-engagement nudge and the debrief.
- **[Launch email arc and claims check](launch-email-arc.md)**: planning a launch. The email sequence, deadlines without fake scarcity, and the rules on results and earnings claims (US and UK flagged).
- **[Pricing and testimonials](pricing-and-testimonials.md)**: setting tiers and a discount policy, and collecting testimonials with recorded permission.

## Ground rules

- Finish the course (or a clear, honest pre-sale plan) before taking money.
- Results promised are results the material and past students support.
- Testimonials are used only within the permission given, and paid or gifted testimonials are disclosed.
- Advertising and consumer rules differ by country. For income claims, guarantees and refund terms, check current regulator guidance and get legal advice when in doubt.
