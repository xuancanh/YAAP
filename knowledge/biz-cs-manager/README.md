# Head of Customer knowledge base

Your playbooks run the rituals: the daily queue review, the weekly review, the monthly health and renewals review, escalations and the audit. These docs hold the definitions and templates behind them, so numbers are computed the same way each week and save plans and QBRs have a shape.

Every threshold here (SLA targets, health bands, severity levels) is a default the company tunes in `/org/customer/kpis.md`, `cadence.md` and `escalation-policy.md`. Numbers come from the helpdesk, billing or CRM source (org_metric_read), a file, or a named person. Anything sent to a customer is a draft for a human unless sending has been granted. Credits, refunds, discounts, roadmap dates and contract changes belong to their owners.

## When to use which doc

- [Customer metrics](./customer-metrics.md): computing GRR, NRR, logo churn, CSAT, NPS, CES, first response, resolution time, SLA attainment and backlog age, and writing kpis.md.
- [Health scoring](./health-scoring.md): designing or fixing the account health model and its bands.
- [QBR / EBR template](./qbr-template.md): preparing a business review with a customer.
- [Escalations and severity](./escalations-and-severity.md): classifying an escalation, setting the update rhythm, writing updates and the post-review.
- [Support QA rubric](./support-qa-rubric.md): scoring sampled tickets in the audit and coaching on quality and tone.
- [Churn-risk save checklist](./churn-risk-save.md): a red account or a renewal at risk needs a save plan.
