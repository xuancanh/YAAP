# School paperwork workflow

Most school paperwork is one of five things. Sort each item into its type, pull out the date and the action, then file it. Nothing should stay "to read later".

## The five types

| Type | Examples | What to pull out |
|---|---|---|
| **Action with deadline** | Consent forms, trip slips, payments, club sign-ups | Deadline, cost, who signs, how to return |
| **Date to remember** | Concerts, parents' evenings, non-uniform days, closures | Date, time, what to bring or wear |
| **Supplies** | Ingredients for food tech, costumes, stationery lists | What, by when, who buys |
| **Information** | Newsletters, policies, term dates | Any hidden dates or actions; otherwise file |
| **Records** | Reports, assessments, medical or support plans | File; note review dates |

## Intake steps

1. **Capture everything in one place.** Photos of paper letters, forwarded emails, screenshots from school apps.
2. **Read for dates and money first.** Newsletters often bury a deadline in paragraph four.
3. **Create the task:** "Sign and return Year 4 trip form + payment — due Fri 14 Nov — Adult A."
4. **Add calendar entries** for events, with reminders the day before for anything to bring.
5. **Add to the week-ahead prep list** if it falls in the next fortnight.
6. **File it** by child and school year: `school/<child>/<year>/`. Keep a running "key dates" file per child.

## Weekly checks

- [ ] Any form or payment due this week?
- [ ] Any non-uniform, dress-up or bring-something days?
- [ ] Any early finishes, closures or training days that change childcare?
- [ ] Anything waiting on the other adult's signature?

## Term start checklist

- [ ] Term dates and closure days in the calendar for the whole year if available.
- [ ] Timetable: PE days, club days, swimming.
- [ ] Contact details and medical information up to date with the school.
- [ ] Lunch arrangements and accounts topped up.
- [ ] Uniform and supplies list checked against what fits.
- [ ] Logins for school apps and payment systems stored securely.

## Sensitive records

Reports, support plans, medical notes and safeguarding letters stay in private files, not the shared family folder, unless the adults decide otherwise. Older children may deserve privacy about some records too.

## Worked example

A Friday newsletter mentions: (paragraph 2) a book fair next Thursday, (paragraph 4) flu vaccine consent due Monday, (paragraph 5) Christmas jumper day on 12 December. Output: task "Flu consent form — due Mon — Adult B"; calendar "Book fair Thu, send money if wanted"; calendar "Christmas jumper day 12 Dec" with a reminder a week before to check the jumper still fits.

## Common mistakes

- Reading the headline and missing the buried deadline.
- One adult receives all school email and becomes the bottleneck. Add both to school lists where possible.
- Paper forms signed but left in a bag.
- No record of what was paid, which causes double payments.
