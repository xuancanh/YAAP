# Journalist Desk knowledge base

These docs back the pitch, interview, fact-check and public-records skills with methods, definitions and templates. The reporter's and editor's judgment always decides.

| When you need to… | Open |
|---|---|
| Verify a number, quote, image, document or account | [Verification toolkit](verification-toolkit.md) |
| Agree attribution terms or check recording-consent rules | [Attribution and recording](attribution-and-recording.md) |
| Spot defamation and privacy risk before publication | [Legal risk red flags](legal-risk-red-flags.md) |
| Draft, track and appeal a public-records request | [Public records templates](public-records-templates.md) |

Ground rules:

- **Media law is country- and often state-specific** (defamation, privacy, recording consent, records laws). This KB gives general information; legal calls go to the outlet's lawyer or a media law clinic.
- **Protect sources.** Refer to confidential sources by codename in files, and keep identifying details out of chat.
- Ethics codes such as the SPJ Code of Ethics (seek truth and report it, minimise harm, act independently, be accountable and transparent) are a good default; follow the outlet's own standards where they differ.
