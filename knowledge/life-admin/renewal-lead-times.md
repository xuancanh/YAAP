# Renewal lead times

The goal is a reminder early enough to act calmly, not a reminder on the day it expires. Lead time = processing time + time to gather what's needed + a buffer for things going wrong.

**Country-specific:** processing times for passports, ID cards, driving licences and visas vary by country and season, and change. Look up the current official processing time when you set the reminder, note the date you checked, and re-check when the reminder fires.

## Typical starting points

| Item | Start the reminder ladder | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Passport | Months ahead, plus any trip | Many countries require the passport to be valid for a period beyond your travel dates (often several months). Check the destination's entry rules. Processing can slow sharply before holiday seasons. |
| National ID, residence permit, visa | Months ahead | Appointments may be booked out; some can only be renewed in a set window. Check the official rules. |
| Driving licence | 2–3 months | Some need a photo, medical form or eye test. |
| Car registration, road tax, inspection | 1 month | Often needs an inspection or insurance proof first. |
| Home, car, travel insurance | 4–6 weeks | Time to read the renewal notice and compare quotes before it auto-renews. |
| Subscriptions with annual billing | 2–3 weeks | Cancellation often has to happen before the renewal date, sometimes days before. |
| Free trials | 2–3 days before end | They convert to paid automatically. |
| Warranties | 1 month before end | Time to report a fault while still covered. |
| Professional licences, certifications | Per body's rules | Continuing-education hours may need months. |
| Medical: prescriptions, check-ups, vaccinations | Per clinician | Store the next-due date the clinician gave; don't calculate one. |

## The reminder ladder

One reminder gets snoozed. A ladder works:

1. **Heads-up** at the lead time: "Passport expires in six months. Renewal currently takes about X (checked <date>). Want me to prep the checklist?"
2. **Action** when it's time to apply: the checklist (forms, photo, fees, documents).
3. **Last call** at a fixed buffer before expiry, if it's still open.
4. **Confirm** when done: record new expiry, remove the ladder, set the next one.

## Hidden dependencies

Many renewals need something else first. Note these in the tracker:

- Passport photo that meets current rules
- Proof of address (a recent bill or statement)
- An old document to send back
- An inspection or test before the renewal
- An insurance certificate before registration
- A child's passport needing both parents' consent

## Worked example

Driving licence expires 15 March. The user says the local office takes about three weeks and needs an eye test.

- 15 Dec: heads-up, book eye test
- 5 Jan: action, gather photo and eye test result, apply
- 15 Feb: last call if not submitted
- On receipt: log new expiry, next ladder starts in ten years minus lead time

## Common mistakes

- Setting the reminder on the expiry date.
- Forgetting the passport validity rule for trips.
- Trusting auto-renewal: it can lapse when a card expires.
- Not recording the new expiry after renewal, so the tracker silently goes stale.
