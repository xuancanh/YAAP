# The admin hour

Small tasks are rarely hard. They pile up because each one feels too small to start. A fixed, weekly hour turns the pile into one job.

## The setup

- **Same slot every week.** A recurring calendar block: for example, Sunday 10:00.
- **One inbox.** Everything that comes up during the week (letters, "must call the dentist", returns) goes into one list. Not spread across notes, texts and email flags.
- **Everything to hand:** the list, calendar, documents folder, a pen for forms.

## Triage first (10 minutes)

Go through the list once. Each item gets one tag:

| Tag | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Now** | Can be finished in this hour | Pay a bill, book an appointment, reply to a form |
| **Errand** | Needs a trip out | Return a parcel, pick up a prescription |
| **Project** | Needs more than one sitting | Switch energy supplier, sort out a pension |
| **Drop** | No longer matters | The class you didn't sign up for in time |

Projects get a *next step* that fits the hour ("find the last three bills"), not the whole thing.

## Work in this order

1. **Anything with a deadline this week.** Late fees and missed windows cost the most.
2. **Phone calls.** Batch them while offices are open. Have reference numbers ready.
3. **Online forms and payments.**
4. **Filing.** What came in this week, into the right folder.
5. **Plan errands.** Group by place and opening hours.
6. **Look ahead:** next week's calendar, anything to prepare.

Stop at the hour. Unfinished items roll over with a note on where you stopped.

## Planning an errand run

- Group by area, then order by route, not by importance.
- Check opening hours. Post offices, pharmacies and council offices often close earlier than shops or at lunch.
- Put time-sensitive or cold items last (groceries, prescriptions that need the fridge).
- Bring everything for each stop: receipts, return labels, ID, bags.

**Worked example.** Errands: return shoes (high street), pick up prescription (pharmacy near home), post a parcel (post office, high street), buy a birthday card, drop off dry cleaning (out of town).

Route: dry cleaning first (furthest), then high street: post office and shoe return and card shop together, then pharmacy on the way home. Four errands, one loop, about an hour instead of three separate trips.

## Keeping it going

- Missed a week? Do a short one, deadlines only. Don't skip twice.
- If the hour never finishes, the list is carrying projects pretending to be tasks. Move them out.
- Note what kept recurring. Some tasks can be automated with direct debits or standing orders, or cancelled.

## Common mistakes

- No single inbox, so the hour starts with hunting.
- Starting with the satisfying tasks instead of the deadlines.
- Treating "sort out pension" as one task.
- Letting the hour stretch to three. Keep it short so it stays a habit.
