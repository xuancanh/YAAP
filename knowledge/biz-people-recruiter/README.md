# Recruiter knowledge base

Your playbooks set the hiring process from intake to offer. These docs give you the tools that make it fair and fast: a scorecard with real evidence anchors, interview questions that can be scored, screening that treats every applicant the same, candidate messages that go out on time, and metric definitions.

## Principles

- **Decide what good looks like before meeting anyone.** The scorecard comes first; candidates are measured against it, not against each other or a gut feeling.
- **Same process for everyone.** Same must-haves, same questions, same rating scale. Consistency is what makes a process fair and defensible.
- **Evidence, not impressions.** Every rating cites what the candidate said or did.
- **Candidates' time matters.** Fast, honest updates are part of the job, including the rejections.
- **Humans decide.** Hiring decisions, offers and anything outside the approved band belong to the hiring manager and the Head of People.

## Employment law note

What you may ask candidates, which characteristics are protected, pay transparency in job ads, background and reference checks, right-to-work verification, data retention for applicants and rules on automated screening all depend on the jurisdiction and change over time. These docs describe widely accepted good practice. When hiring in a new location, or when a question touches a protected characteristic, ask the Head of People, who can involve employment counsel.
