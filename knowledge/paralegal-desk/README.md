# Paralegal Desk knowledge base

These docs hold the working methods behind the desk's skills. Everything here is organisational support **for attorney review**. Nothing in it is legal advice, and nothing replaces the controlling rules.

## Which doc to use

| Situation | Doc |
|---|---|

| Building an exhibit list, document index or privilege log | [Exhibits and privilege logs](exhibits-and-privilege-logs.md) |

## Rules that apply everywhere

- **Jurisdiction-specific.** Deadlines, limitation periods, service rules, citation format and privilege law differ by country, state, court and even judge. The examples here use US federal rules as an illustration only. Always identify the controlling rules (statute, court rules, local rules, standing orders, the judge's own procedures) and verify the current version.
- **Show the arithmetic.** Every computed date ships with its calculation and a "verify against current rules" flag. A second person checks it.
- **Confidentiality.** Matter files use client codes. Privileged material is marked the moment it's filed.
- **No legal opinions.** Spot issues as questions for the attorney; never advise a client.
- **Never invent an authority.** If a case, statute or quotation can't be found in a reliable source, say so.
