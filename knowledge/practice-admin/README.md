# Practice Admin knowledge base

Administrative reference for the front desk of a small clinical practice. Nothing here is clinical: no symptoms, treatments or urgency judgments. Anything clinical goes to the practitioner or to the practice's agreed "please call us / seek urgent care" wording.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Privacy at the front desk](privacy-at-the-front-desk.md) | Writing any reminder, form, sign-in process or file name; answering "can we say this?" |
| [Schedule design](schedule-design.md) | Building the appointment template, buffers, waitlist and no-show rules |
| [Recall system method](recall-system-method.md) | Setting up due-date tracking and the recall outreach ladder |
| [Fee conversations and hard messages](fees-and-hard-messages.md) | Estimates, overdue balances, policy changes and replying to public reviews |

Privacy rules are country-specific. The privacy doc gives the US (HIPAA) and UK (UK GDPR) basics as general orientation. The practice's own privacy officer, professional body or a healthcare lawyer decides anything specific. Every patient-facing draft goes to the practitioner before it ships.
