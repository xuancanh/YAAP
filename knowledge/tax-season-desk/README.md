# Tax Season Desk knowledge base

Workflow references for a tax preparer, bookkeeper or small accounting practice. These docs organise the season. None of them takes a tax position: what's deductible, which election to make or how anything is treated is the qualified preparer's judgment, and any draft touching substance carries the **for preparer review** flag.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Client document checklists](client-document-checklists.md) | Building a per-client checklist from their profile at season start |
| [Missing-docs chase ladder](missing-docs-chase-ladder.md) | Writing and scheduling reminders for outstanding documents |
| [Workpaper structure](workpaper-structure.md) | Organising a return's file for fast review; tracking review notes |
| [Deadline calendar](deadline-calendar.md) | Setting up the filing calendar and lead times (US and UK reference dates) |
| [Client data security](client-data-security.md) | Handling documents safely; what never goes in this desk's files |

Tax rules, forms and dates are **country- and year-specific** and change. Dates and form names here are reference points checked on 2026-11-04 against IRS and GOV.UK pages. Always confirm the current year on the official source before relying on them.
