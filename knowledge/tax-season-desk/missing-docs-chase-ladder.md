# Missing-docs chase ladder

Most late returns are late because documents are late. A predictable ladder, polite and escalating, gets them in without anyone having to remember to chase.

## The ladder

| Step | When | Channel | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Request | Checklist sent at season start | Email / portal | Friendly, full list |
| 2. Reminder | ~2 weeks after request, or when items should be available | Email / portal | Short, only what's outstanding |
| 3. Nudge | ~2 weeks later | Text or call, plus email | Direct, with the date by which the practice needs them |
| 4. Deadline notice | Before the practice's internal cutoff | Email from the preparer | Clear consequence: extension or late filing (preparer decides wording) |

Adjust spacing to the calendar: compress as the filing deadline approaches. Stop the ladder the moment everything is in, and send a thank-you.

## Rules

- **List only outstanding items**, by name, never the whole checklist again.
- **No tax IDs, account numbers or amounts** in any message. Name the document, not its contents.
- **Secure upload only.** Ask for documents through the practice's portal, never by email attachment.
- **Never tell the client what a missing item means for their tax.** "Your return can't be finished without it" is fine; "you'll lose the deduction" is substance, for the preparer.
- Step 4 always goes to the preparer before sending.
- Log every touch: date, step, channel.

## Templates

**Step 1: request**

> Hi [first name], it's tax season again. Here's the list of documents we need for your [year] return: [list]. Upload them to your portal at [link] as they arrive. Some forms aren't issued until later in the year, so send what you have now. Questions? Just reply.

**Step 2: reminder**

> Hi [first name], thanks for the documents so far. We're still waiting on: [items]. Upload to [link] when you can.

**Step 3: nudge**

> Hi [first name], quick one: we still need [items] to finish your return. Could you upload them by [date]? If something's missing or delayed, let us know and we'll talk about options.

**Step 4: deadline notice (preparer approves)**

> Hi [first name], we haven't yet received [items]. To file on time we need them by [date]. If we don't have them by then, [preparer's chosen wording, e.g. we'll talk to you about filing an extension]. Please call us on [number] if there's a problem.

## Special cases

- **Late-issued forms** (e.g. partnership K-1s, some broker statements): set the expected date on the checklist and don't chase before it.
- **Client says they never received a form:** note it and send to the preparer; obtaining a copy from the issuer or the tax authority is their advice to give.
- **Non-responsive client:** after step 4, the preparer decides. Don't keep messaging.
- **Client sends documents by plain email:** thank them, file them securely, delete the email per practice policy, and remind them about the portal.

## Measuring

Weekly during the season: number of clients complete, waiting on one item, waiting on several, not started. Clients stuck on the same item for two steps go on the preparer's list.

## Common mistakes

- Resending the full checklist, so the client can't see what's actually missing.
- Asking for documents by email attachment.
- Reminders that drift into tax advice.
