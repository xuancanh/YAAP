# Deadline calendar

**Dates are year-specific and change.** When a deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday it usually moves, and tax authorities sometimes extend deadlines for disasters or other events. Confirm every date on the official source each season. This doc lists individual-return reference points only; business, payroll, state and local deadlines need their own entries.

## Building the calendar

For each client, list every filing and payment they have, then work backwards with lead times:

| Client | Filing / payment | Official due | Internal docs cutoff | Draft by | Review by | Client sign-off by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Internal dates are the practice's own choice. Common pattern: documents several weeks before the due date, draft two weeks before, review one week before, client approval a few days before. Leave the most time for clients who are always late or have complex returns.

## US individual reference points (federal)

- **Filing due date:** 15 April for calendar-year individuals, moved to the next business day if it falls on a weekend or legal holiday. For tax-year 2026 returns that's normally **15 April 2027**.
- **Extension:** filing Form 4868 by the original due date gives an automatic six-month extension to file (normally **15 October 2027** for tax year 2026). **An extension to file is not an extension to pay.** Tax owed is still due by the original date.
- **Estimated tax:** the year is split into four payment periods, each with its own due date (usually mid-April, mid-June, mid-September and mid-January of the following year). Check the current Form 1040-ES for exact dates. Weekend and holiday dates move to the next business day.
- **State returns:** many follow the federal date, but not all; check each state.

## UK Self Assessment reference points

For the 2025–26 tax year (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026), per GOV.UK:

- **Register** for Self Assessment if new: by **5 October 2026**
- **Paper return:** by **31 October 2026**
- **Online return:** by **31 January 2027** (by 30 December 2026 if the client wants tax collected through their PAYE tax code, where eligible)
- **Pay balance and first payment on account:** **31 January 2027**
- **Second payment on account:** **31 July 2027**

Whether a client has payments on account is decided by HMRC's rules and the preparer.

## Extension and late-risk tracking

- Flag every client still missing documents at the internal cutoff; the preparer decides whether to extend.
- Track extensions filed: date filed, confirmation, new due date.
- Track payments estimated by the preparer for extended clients (the client pays; the desk doesn't calculate).

## Off-season rhythm

- Quarterly: estimated or advance payment reminders, with amounts set by the preparer.
- Extended returns: a second mini-season before the extended due date.
- Year-end: life-change questionnaire, engagement letters, next season's calendar.

## Weekly radar during the season

1. All due dates in the next 30 days
2. Clients past the internal docs cutoff
3. Returns waiting in review
4. Returns waiting for client sign-off
5. Extensions to decide

## Common mistakes

- Copying last year's dates without checking weekends, holidays or rule changes.
- Treating an extension as more time to pay.
- No internal cutoffs, so everything lands in the final week.
