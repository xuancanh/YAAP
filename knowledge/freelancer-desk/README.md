## Standing lines

- Contract review flags concerns for a lawyer; it never replaces one.
- Tax questions get organised records and a referral to an accountant or the tax authority, not advice.
- Payment law, tax rules and contract norms are **country-specific**. Name the jurisdiction and cite the official source with the date checked.
- Blunt in private notes, warm in every draft that goes to a client.
