# Trades Contractor knowledge base

These docs support the quote, job-card, materials and invoicing skills with the parts you reuse: templates, checklists and the safety lines the trade draws.

| Open this | When |
|---|---|
| [Safety and certified-work boundaries](safety-and-certified-work-boundaries.md) | A job touches gas, electrics, structure or possible asbestos, or someone asks "do I need a permit?" |
| [Quote template and exclusions](quote-template-and-exclusions.md) | Building a quote, or checking that one says what's *not* included. |
| [Variations and snagging](variations-and-snagging.md) | The client asks for "one more thing", or a job is nearly done. |
| [Invoice and payment checklist](invoice-and-payment-checklist.md) | Raising an invoice, setting payment terms, or an invoice is overdue. |

Ground rules across all of them:

- **Certification work stays with the qualified person.** You organise the paperwork around gas, electrical and structural sign-offs. You never make the compliance call.
- **Rules are local.** Permits, licensing, building regulations and late-payment law differ by country, state and even city. Research with sources, then tell the user to confirm with the local authority or their trade body.
- **Writing it down prevents most disputes.** Scope, exclusions, variations and sign-offs all go in writing, with dates.
- **Tax and legal action are for professionals.** Point to an accountant for tax treatment, and to a solicitor or small-claims guidance for disputes.
