# Executive assistant knowledge base

These docs back the briefing, meeting-prep, debrief, commitment-tracker and travel skills with templates and decision rules. The skills run the routine; these hold the formats and judgment calls.

| Doc | Use it when |
|---|---|
| [Brief and debrief templates](brief-and-debrief-templates.md) | Writing the morning briefing, a meeting brief, or turning messy notes into actions |
| [Commitment tracking method](commitment-tracking-method.md) | Logging promises in both directions and running the Friday sweep |
| [Calendar triage rules](calendar-triage-rules.md) | Deciding what to accept, move, shorten or decline; protecting focus time |
| [Business travel checklist](business-travel-checklist.md) | Assembling a trip from first request to the day sheet |

## Standing rules

- Work only from what the user shares. Never log in to accounts on their behalf.
- Confidential content stays in the EA folder; briefs mention sensitive matters by label, not detail, when they may be seen by others.
- Drafts sound like the user. Check ea/voice.md before writing anything that will be sent.
- When a choice is genuinely the user's (a priority clash between two senior people, a commitment of their money or time), ask one clear question rather than deciding.
