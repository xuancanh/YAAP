# Business travel checklist

A good business trip has one itinerary file, every booking confirmed in writing, and a day sheet per day that answers "where am I going next and how?"

## On the first request

- [ ] Purpose, must-attend meetings and their times; any flexibility on dates.
- [ ] Traveller preferences (from the profile): airline, seat, hotel style, loyalty numbers, dietary needs.
- [ ] Company travel policy and approval needs.
- [ ] Budget or cost centre.

## Before booking

- [ ] **Entry requirements:** passport validity, visa or electronic travel authorisation, and any health requirements. These are **country-specific and change**. Check the destination government's official site and the traveller's own government travel advice, and note the date checked. The traveller or their company's travel provider confirms; the assistant does not decide eligibility.
- [ ] Time zones and jet lag: avoid key meetings straight after a long overnight flight if possible.
- [ ] Local holidays that close offices or affect transport.
- [ ] Travel advisories for the destination.

## Final 48 hours

- [ ] Check-in done; boarding passes saved.
- [ ] Any schedule changes from airlines or hosts.
- [ ] Meeting briefs in the trip folder.
- [ ] Out-of-office and delegation arranged.
- [ ] Payment cards notified if the bank requires it; some local currency if useful.
- [ ] Phone roaming or local data plan.

## Packing for work trips

Documents (passport, visa, ID, insurance details), laptop and chargers, plug adapter, presentation on a backup device or cloud, business cards, one outfit more formal than expected, any medicines in hand luggage with their original labels.

## After the trip

- [ ] Receipts gathered for expenses within policy deadlines.
- [ ] Debriefs for each meeting; commitments logged.
- [ ] Thank-you notes drafted.
- [ ] Loyalty points and traveller preferences updated.

## Common mistakes

- Times written in the traveller's home time zone.
- Non-refundable bookings made before the meetings are confirmed.
- Visa or authorisation checked too late.
- No buffer between landing and the first meeting.
