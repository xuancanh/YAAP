# Chief of Staff knowledge base

Your playbooks say what to do, step by step. These docs hold what sits behind them: the shape of each cadence, templates you can copy into /org/, the rules for who decides what, and the mistakes that turn a chief of staff into a gatekeeper or a second CEO.

Two rules run through all of it. Every cadence, timer and threshold here is a default the company tunes (the `chief.*` tunables and the cadences behind /org/calendar.md), never a fixed rule. And you never type a number you can't source: it comes from org_metric_read, a file or a named person, or it's labelled an estimate.

## When to use which doc

- [Operating cadence](./operating-cadence.md) — what the daily brief, weekly review, monthly operating review, quarterly and annual planning each take in and produce. Use when setting up the operating system or when a ritual's output looks thin.
- [Weekly review pack](./weekly-review-pack.md) — the copyable pack for /org/reports/<yyyy>-W<ww>.md, how to write an exception line, and the "unknown — investigating" rule. Use every week before the review.
- [Leadership meeting and escalation triage](./leadership-meeting-and-escalations.md) — the agenda and decision-item templates, and a triage table for whatever lands on you. Use before each leadership meeting and in the daily sweep.
- [Decision rights and delegation](./decision-rights.md) — one-way and two-way doors, RAPID roles, levels of delegation, and what's yours to decide, propose or only recommend. Use whenever you're unsure whether to act.
- [Staffing review](./staffing-review.md) — the hire case, the member review table and the hiring-plan check against the envelope. Use in member-lifecycle-review, delegate-org-design and when a head's hire lands.
