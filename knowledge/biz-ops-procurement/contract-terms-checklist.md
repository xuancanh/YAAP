# Contract terms checklist

You are not the company's lawyer, and you do not accept terms. Your job is to read the commercial terms carefully, record what matters in the register, and send the right questions to Legal and IT & Security before a contract is recommended.

**Jurisdiction note:** rules on automatic renewal, consumer and business protections, data transfer and liability differ by country and change over time. Treat this checklist as what to look for, not what is enforceable. Legal decides that.

## Commercial terms (record in the register)

- [ ] **Parties and entity:** the vendor's legal entity and ours are named correctly.
- [ ] **Term:** start date, length, and whether it is fixed or month-to-month.
- [ ] **Renewal:** auto-renews yes or no; renewal term length (some renew for the full original term).
- [ ] **Notice period:** how many days before renewal you must give notice, and how (email, portal, written letter to an address). Record the method; a notice sent the wrong way may not count.
- [ ] **Price and uplift:** current price; whether renewal pricing is capped, fixed, or "then-current list price".
- [ ] **Billing:** cycle, payment terms, currency, taxes added or included.
- [ ] **Quantity rules:** can seats go down at renewal or mid-term, or only up? Is there a minimum commitment? How is overage billed?
- [ ] **Termination:** for convenience (can we leave early, at what cost) and for cause (what breach lets us leave).
- [ ] **Order of precedence:** which document wins if the order form and the terms disagree.

## Service terms

- [ ] **Service levels:** uptime or response commitments, how they are measured, and what credits apply. Note whether credits must be claimed and by when.
- [ ] **Support:** hours, channels, and the tier included in this plan.
- [ ] **Changes:** can the vendor change features or terms during the contract, and with what notice?

## Data and security (route to IT & Security)

- [ ] **Data handled:** what company or customer data the tool will hold.
- [ ] **Data processing terms:** whether a data processing agreement is offered or needed.
- [ ] **Location and sub-processors:** where data is stored and who else processes it.
- [ ] **Security evidence:** what the vendor provides (for example audit reports or a security questionnaire). Do not judge its sufficiency yourself; record what exists and when it was reviewed.
- [ ] **Access:** SSO, user provisioning, audit logs — and which plan tier includes them.
- [ ] **Breach notice:** whether the vendor commits to notify us, and how.

## Exit (often forgotten)

- [ ] **Data export:** format and method; whether it is available after termination and for how long.
- [ ] **Deletion:** whether the vendor deletes our data at the end, and confirms it.
- [ ] **Transition help:** any paid or free assistance.

## Risk terms (route to Legal)

- [ ] Liability caps and exclusions.
- [ ] Indemnities.
- [ ] Governing law and venue.
- [ ] Assignment (can the vendor transfer the contract if it is acquired?).
- [ ] Unusual clauses: exclusivity, publicity rights, non-solicitation, audit rights over us.

## How to record findings

Add a short block to the purchase file or renewal note:

```
Contract flags — <vendor> — read <date>
- Auto-renews for 12 months; notice 60 days by email to <address>.
- Renewal at then-current list price (no cap) → negotiation ask.
- Seats can only increase mid-term → right-size before renewal.
- DPA offered; sub-processor list linked → IT & Security review requested <date>.
- Liability clause unusual → Legal review requested <date>.
```

Copy the renewal date, notice period, notice method and auto-renew flag into the register the same day. Those four fields drive the renewal tracker; an error there is how renewals happen by accident.

## Pitfalls

- Reading only the order form. The linked online terms often hold the renewal and uplift clauses.
- Assuming online terms are fixed. Note the version or date you read.
- Missing a click-through acceptance by a requester, which may have created a contract already. Ask how the free trial was started.
