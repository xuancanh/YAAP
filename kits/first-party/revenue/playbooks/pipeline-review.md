---
name: pipeline-review
description: Review every open deal with its owner: stage evidence, next step, risk. Use weekly.
---

1. Read the pipeline summary and the open deals (crm operations), largest first.
2. For each deal: is the stage backed by evidence, is there a next step with a date, what could kill it?
3. Move stages that changed and log the activities that changed them.
4. Close out deals silent for a month as lost with the reason.
5. Report pipeline value, the deals that moved and the three deals that decide the month.
