---
name: queue-review
description: Review the support queue: breaches, urgent tickets, the oldest waits and the owner of each. Use every working morning.
---

1. Read the queue stats and the open tickets (support operations).
2. Every breach gets an owner and a reply drafted today; every urgent ticket gets an owner within the hour.
3. Tickets waiting on the customer for more than five days get a polite nudge drafted, or are solved.
4. Write three lines for the daily report: open, breaching, the oldest wait and why.
