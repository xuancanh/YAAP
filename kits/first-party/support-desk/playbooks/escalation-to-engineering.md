---
name: escalation-to-engineering
description: Hand a real bug from a ticket to engineering with everything they need, and keep the customer informed. Use when a ticket describes broken behaviour.
---

1. Reproduce or confirm the problem from the ticket; quote the customer, never paste their personal data.
2. Create a task for the engineering head with the ticket id, steps, expected and actual behaviour, and the number of customers affected.
3. Draft a reply telling the customer it is with engineering and when they will hear back.
4. When engineering closes the task, draft the follow-up and close the ticket.
