---
name: nda-turnaround
description: Turn around a standard NDA in a day: compare to the company template, redline the differences, and hand it to the owner to sign. Use when a counterparty sends an NDA.
---

1. Compare the NDA to the company template clause by clause; mark the differences.
2. Accept differences within the template's fallbacks; redline the rest with the template wording.
3. Run the contract checklist on the task and note anything beyond policy.
4. Hand the redlined NDA and a three-line summary to the owner, who signs; agents never sign.
