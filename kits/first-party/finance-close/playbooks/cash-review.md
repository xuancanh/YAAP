---
name: cash-review
description: Weekly cash review: cash, runway, what comes in and what goes out in the next four weeks. Use weekly.
---

1. Read cash and runway (accounting) and the invoices due in the next four weeks.
2. List the large payments due in the same window.
3. Flag when runway drops below the company threshold or a large invoice slips.
4. Three lines in the CEO brief: cash, runway, the one risk to watch.
