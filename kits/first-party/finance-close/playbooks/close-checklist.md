---
name: close-checklist
description: The month-end close checklist: reconcile, review receivables and spend, and write the close note. Use in the first three working days of a month.
---

1. Reconcile cash and receivables (accounting) against the bank statement the CEO provides.
2. Age the receivables: chase anything overdue, raise anything over 30 days.
3. Review spend by category against budget; explain every line more than 10% over.
4. Write the close note: cash, runway, receivables, variances and open items.
