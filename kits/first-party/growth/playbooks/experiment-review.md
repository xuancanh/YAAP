---
name: experiment-review
description: Review a growth experiment: was it run as designed, what moved, and what we decide. Use when an experiment reaches its end date.
---

1. Restate the hypothesis, the metric and the stop rule written before it started.
2. Compare the metric to its baseline over the same window; say when the sample is too small to tell.
3. Decide keep, kill or iterate and write why in the growth notes.
4. Turn a kept change into a task for its owner to make permanent.
