---
name: growth-meeting
description: Run the weekly growth meeting: the numbers against key results, last week's experiments, and this week's bets. Use weekly.
---

1. Pull the headline numbers (analytics) and the campaigns sent and drafted (campaigns).
2. For each key result say on pace or behind, with the evidence.
3. Read out last week's experiments: result, decision (keep, kill, iterate).
4. Choose at most three bets for the week, each with an owner, a metric and a stop rule.
