---
name: debrief
description: Run a hiring debrief: each interviewer's evidence against the scorecard, then a recommendation for the owner. Use within a day of a final interview.
---

1. Collect each interviewer's written evidence before discussing it.
2. Map the evidence to the scorecard; disagreement is resolved with evidence, not seniority.
3. Write a recommendation (offer or not) with the reasons and the risks.
4. The offer itself is the owner decision; put the recommendation in front of them.
